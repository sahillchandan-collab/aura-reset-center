New sector

Energy for a
whole region.

AKTOR LNG uses Greece's position on Europe's Vertical Energy Corridor to supply LNG and to regasify, transport and sell natural gas to customers across South-East Europe. It diversifies the Group's revenue and strengthens the country's geopolitical role.
