TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Agreement no. YSA-
  1. Payment in advance. Each month's fee is due before that month starts. Yontro may pause all work, including posting, while a payment is overdue. If a payment is 14 days overdue, Yontro may end this agreement by written notice, and fees for the rest of the committed term remain payable.
  2. Payment methods. Cash is valid only against a Yontro receipt or invoice signed and stamped by Yontro. Card payments require the terminal receipt. Cheques are payable to “Yontro - F.Z.E”; a returned cheque must be replaced within 3 working days, and bank charges for it are paid by the Client.
  3. Refunds and missed deliverables. Fees paid are non-refundable. If Yontro delivers fewer videos or posts than the package includes in a month, the shortfall is delivered the following month or credited against the next invoice; anything still owed when the agreement ends is refunded at the package price per video.
  4. Ad spend and VAT. Budgets for paid campaigns are paid by the Client directly to the ad platforms and are not part of Yontro's fee. VAT at 5% is added only if Yontro is VAT-registered and its TRN is shown on this agreement.
  5. Commitment term. The Client commits to the term ticked in section 3 (standard: 3 months). If the Client ends the agreement during the committed term, the fees for the rest of that term remain payable.
  6. After the term. When the committed term ends, the agreement continues month to month on the same fee, paid in advance, until either party gives 30 days' written notice (WhatsApp or email to the contacts in this agreement is accepted).
  7. What the Client provides. Within 7 days of signing: logo, photos, price list or menu, access to the agreed accounts, and one point of contact who can approve content. The Client makes the agreed people available for scheduled shoots.
  8. Approvals and changes. The Client sees every video before it goes live; nothing is posted without approval. The Client approves or comments within 48 hours. Two rounds of changes per video are included; further changes or reshoots are quoted as add-ons.
  9. Shoots. Shoots are scheduled in advance at the Client's premises in Dubai. A shoot cancelled by the Client with less than 24 hours' notice may be counted as delivered. Shoots in Sharjah, Ajman and Abu Dhabi carry a travel charge quoted per shoot.
  10. Delays caused by the Client. If materials, access or approvals are late, Yontro's delivery dates move by the same number of days. This does not reduce or delay the fees.
  11. Ownership. The Client owns the final delivered videos, designs and website for its business use once the related fees are paid. Yontro keeps its methods, templates and raw footage, and may show the work in its portfolio unless the Client objects in writing.
  12. The Client's accounts. The Client's social media accounts, pages and domain remain the Client's property. When the agreement ends, Yontro returns any access it holds.
  13. Results. Yontro commits to the strategy, quality and quantity of work in the chosen package. Views, followers, enquiries and sales depend on factors outside Yontro's control; they are targets, not guarantees.
  14. Referrals. Referral discounts under Yontro's referral programme apply to Yontro's fee only, are not exchangeable for cash, and are applied as set out in the Client Starter Pack.
  15. Confidentiality. Each party keeps the other's business information confidential during and after this agreement.
  16. Liability. Yontro's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees the Client paid in the 3 months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect loss or loss of profit.
  17. Whole agreement. This agreement, the package described in section 2 and the Client Starter Pack policies form the whole agreement. If they conflict, this agreement prevails. Changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.
  18. Law. This agreement is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates, and disputes go to the competent courts of the UAE. Each party keeps one signed copy.
