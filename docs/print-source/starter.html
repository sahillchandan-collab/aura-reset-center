How we work

One visit. One strategy. One shoot day. Then we post.

01

The visit

Our strategist comes to your business and runs our Get Viral Blueprint questionnaire: what you sell, who buys it, and why they choose you over the place next door.
02

The strategy

From the blueprint we write your three-month plan: storylines, hooks, formats and a posting schedule built for results, not just activity.
03

The shoot day

Our creative director shoots the month's videos in a single day at your premises. One day of your time, a month of finished content.
04

The posting

Your account manager posts on schedule, replies to comments, forwards every enquiry to you and sends the monthly report.

The seven-day rule

Habits are built in seven days. In your first week we post every single day, so your audience and the algorithm both learn you are here. Then we keep the rhythm for you, so it never depends on a busy Tuesday.

Our policies

The short version of our contract.

These policies summarise the Yontro service agreement you signed. If anything here differs from the signed agreement, the agreement applies.

  1. TermMinimum three months from the start date. After month three the agreement continues month to month, and either side may end it with 30 days' written notice.
  2. Fees and invoicingFees are monthly in AED: Launch 5,000, Growth 7,500, Scale 10,000. Add-ons are quoted and agreed in writing before work starts.
  3. Pay on deliveryWe invoice at the end of each month for that month's delivered work. You pay within five days. If we deliver fewer videos than agreed, that month's fee is reduced pro rata.
  4. Payment methodsCash, card or cheque. Cash is valid only against a signed and stamped Yontro invoice. A returned cheque must be replaced within three working days, with bank charges paid by the client.
  5. Ad spendBudgets for paid campaigns are paid by you directly to the ad platforms and are not part of Yontro's fee.
  6. What you provideMaterials, account access and one point of contact within seven days of signing, and people available for scheduled shoots.
  7. Approvals and changesYou see every video before it goes live, and nothing is posted without your approval. Please approve or comment within 48 hours. Two rounds of changes are included per video.
  8. Shoot cancellationsShoots cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice may be counted as delivered.
  9. Who owns the contentOn payment, you own the final videos, designs and website for your business use. Yontro keeps its methods, templates and raw footage, and may show the work in its portfolio unless you object in writing.
  10. ResultsWe commit to the strategy, quality and quantity of work agreed. Views, followers and sales depend on factors outside our control, so they are targets, not guarantees.
  11. Your accountsYour social media accounts and website remain yours. When the agreement ends, we hand back any access we hold.
  12. ConfidentialityEach side keeps the other's business information confidential during and after the agreement.
  13. Shoots outside DubaiOur team shoots across Dubai. Shoots in Sharjah, Ajman and Abu Dhabi are quoted for travel per shoot.
  14. LawThe agreement is governed by the laws of the UAE and the courts of Dubai. Changes must be in writing and signed by both sides.
