Can the studios help pay for a AED 3.5M townhouse?

Yes, partly. But the order of the loans matters more than any other decision in this plan. A bank lends up to 80% on your first home, and only 60% on each property after that. Once the studios carry a mortgage, the townhouse is likely to be treated as an additional property.

The honest answer

The studios cover roughly 49% of the home loan on Route A and 37% on Route B, before any rent increases. They will not pay the whole mortgage on their own; your business income covers the rest. Note that the equity release grows your wealth, not your monthly income: two debt-free studios actually leave more cash each month than four mortgaged ones.

Route B saves about AED 691,250 of cash on the home but needs ~AED 4,123,000 of total borrowing, above today's AED 3.5M pre-approval. Route A fits within it but needs ~AED 1,647,820 in cash for the home.

Why JVT

A value gap with a catalyst next door

Skylight Mall: the stalled mall is back

Nakheel's long-delayed Al Khail Avenue Mall site on Al Khail Road has been relaunched by Dubai Retail (Dubai Holding) as Skylight Mall: about 1.3M sqft, 360+ stores, opening scheduled for 2027, and explicitly positioned to serve JVT, Jumeirah Islands and The Springs. It is walking distance from Al Manara Tower. Its opening lands right around our Year-2 valuation, when the bank values your studios for the release.

Announced 6 Oct 2026 · Khaleej Times · Gulf News

Infrastructure

  • Al Khail Road upgrade (AED 700M) completed in 2024.
  • Hessa Street Phase II (AED 689M, awarded Feb 2026): doubles capacity to Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Road, with a JVC tunnel and ramp.
  • Dubai Metro Gold Line (approved Apr 2026): via Al Barsha South and JVC, target 2032.
  • Airport Express line along Al Khail Road with a JVC station (tendered Apr 2026).

Families and schools

Sunmarke School (British + IB) and Arcadia School are inside JVT; JSS International (JVC) and Nord Anglia International School (Al Barsha South, KHDA Outstanding) are around 12 minutes away. Family demand keeps occupancy high across the community.

Developers still investing

38 new projects are underway in JVT, including Binghatti Luxuria and Danube Fashionz in District 4, the same district as Al Manara, plus ELAR1S, Guzel Towers and Sol Levante. Large plots are still being built out, and new branded stock resets the area's price per sqft upwards, pulling older towers like Al Manara with it.

Why we expect 10% rather than 5%

Al Manara studios trade at ~AED 1,100–1,250/sqft. Newer JVT studios record a median of ~AED 1,830–1,940/sqft. That gap, plus a super-regional mall opening on the doorstep in 2027 and better road and metro links, is the case for valuations rising faster than the 5% floor, and for a stronger bank valuation at the Year-2 release.

Sahill's recommendation

Start debt-free, then let the bank build the second half.

Hashim, buy the two Al Manara studios in cash now. You become the strongest buyer in the building, you earn about AED 6,525 a month from day one, and you carry zero debt while JVT's next chapter (Skylight Mall in 2027, the road and metro upgrades) plays out.

At the end of Year 2 we take the equity release and buy two more studios. Same capital, double the portfolio: around AED 2.2M at the floor, AED 2.4M if JVT moves as we expect, and every unit paying its own mortgage.

Before that release, we sit down with your bank and fix the order for the townhouse. If the bank confirms it will lend 80% on the home only while the studios are debt-free, we mortgage the home first and save you around AED 691,250 in cash. Either way, the studios' surplus covers roughly a third to half of the home loan, and the Year-5 release adds more firepower.
