## Our values in practice

We have four things we will not compromise on.

| Value | What it looks like on shift |
|---|---|
| **The nervous system first** | Every visit starts by asking what state the guest arrived in. You slow down before you speed up. You notice your own state, too. |
| **Clinical hardware only** | We use Elevé Health equipment only, installed and serviced by their technicians. You never adjust, bypass or repair a unit yourself; you report faults immediately. |
| **A host, not a receptionist** | Someone who knows the modalities walks with each guest from arrival to the last exhale. You stay present, explain clearly and never rush a guest out. |
| **Whole person, always** | Body, mind, nervous system and spirit. You treat guests and colleagues with dignity, respect privacy and use wellness language, never medical claims. |

## The Host promise

Every Host commits to five things with every guest:

1. **Meet them.** A warm welcome by name, and a moment to ask how they arrived today.
1. **Breathe with them.** Every visit begins with our ritual: in for four, out for six.
1. **Tailor it.** Build the session around their goal: sleep deeper, calm the stress, recover faster, more energy, skin and glow, sharper focus or beat jet lag.
1. **Keep them safe.** Screening complete, protocol followed, time limits respected, never alone in the cold plunge.
1. **Close gently.** Check how they feel, offer a refreshment, and suggest the next step without pressure.

## The Elevé partnership

Every machine in our studio is Elevé Health, and The Aura is Elevé Health's first partner clinic in the UAE. The Elevé standard rests on four commitments:

1. **Installed by Elevé.**
1. **Calibrated to clinical spec.**
1. **Hosts trained by Elevé.** You will complete Elevé device training for each modality before operating it unsupervised.
1. **Supported for life.** Elevé provides ongoing technical support, servicing on their clinical schedule and protocol updates.

Elevé's own Longevity Lounge is in Newport Beach. When their technicians visit, treat them as partners: give them access, log the visit and share any issues you have noticed.
