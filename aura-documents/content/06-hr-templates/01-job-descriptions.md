# Job Descriptions

> Document No. AUR-HR-01 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

> Draft for internal use. To be reviewed by a UAE-qualified legal adviser once the trade license is issued.

This document sets out the eight founding roles, plus one future role, for the Dubai studio at 332 Jumeirah First, Jumeirah Beach Road, Jumeirah 1, opening November 2026. Each role exists to protect one promise: every guest is met by someone who knows the modalities and walks with them from arrival to the last exhale.

**Applies to all roles.** The studio is open seven days a week, 9:00am–9:00pm. For now the floor runs with 2 Hosts per shift across two shifts a day (planning assumption 9:00–15:30 and 14:30–21:00 [confirm]). Every guest's first visit starts with a Healer consultation. No medical treatments are offered at launch. Shifts are rostered under the Staff rota template (AUR-HR-08) within the working-hours limits of UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021. All staff complete Elevé Health training relevant to their role after hire, current CPR/BLS certification, and the onboarding plan in AUR-HR-05. Salaries and benefits are confirmed in the individual offer letter (AUR-HR-03).

## 1. Lead Host / Operations Lead

**Purpose.** Lead the Host team on the studio floor and keep every shift running to the Elevé standard: safe equipment, calm guests, on-time sessions.

**Reports to:** General Manager. **Direct reports:** Hosts, Spa Attendants (shift supervision of Guest Relations Executives).

### Key responsibilities
- Lead the opening and closing checks for all launch modalities (one unit of each machine), including cryotherapy chamber, cold plunge water temperature (5 °C), sauna, hyperbaric chamber and dry float bed readiness [per Elevé protocol — pending].
- Build and publish the weekly rota; ensure 2 Hosts on every shift and approve shift swaps.
- Host guests personally, especially first-timers booking "Not sure yet — build my reset", and model the Aura guest journey.
- Coach Hosts on protocols, contrast pairings and the opening breathing ritual: "In for four, out for six."
- Act as first point of escalation for guest concerns, incidents and near misses; log them in line with AUR-SAF-01 and AUR-SAF-05.
- Liaise with Elevé Health technicians on service visits, faults and protocol updates; keep the equipment log current.
- Track consumables and stock (robes, Aura-branded towels, clothing and slippers, salt cabin supplies, refreshments and retail stock) and raise orders.
- As senior Host on the closing shift, complete the end-of-day cash close (Apple Pay, card and cash), checked by the General Manager next morning [default — confirm].
- Lead the founding-member welcome and the November launch-week floor plan.

## 2. Host (Wellness Host)

**Purpose.** Be the guest's guide through their reset. A host, not a receptionist: you know the modalities and walk with each guest from arrival to the last exhale.

**Reports to:** Lead Host / Operations Lead.

### Key responsibilities
- Welcome each guest, check how they arrived (the nervous system first), and lead the opening breathing ritual.
- Confirm pre-session screening and consent forms are complete before every session, and that a first-time guest has had their Healer consultation.
- Operate Elevé equipment to protocol [per Elevé protocol — pending]: E-CRYO PRO cryotherapy, red-light bed, infrared sauna, hyperbaric chamber (once signed off as a senior Host), compression boots, dry float bed, E-Salt Cabin and the Polar Recovery cold plunge (always host-guided).
- Prepare and reset treatment rooms between guests, and share cleaning duties on shift.
- Follow the Healer's personalised package, build 3-step sequences for first-timers and suggest combinations and contrast pairings (cold plunge with infrared sauna; compression with sauna or cryotherapy; salt cabin before the dry float bed or breathwork).
- Answer WhatsApp and website enquiries quickly when on shift; many new guests come from paid ad campaigns.
- Serve refreshments, issue robes, Aura-branded towels, clothing and slippers, and introduce retail (copper mugs, organic fragrances, wellness pillows, linen merchandise).
- Take payments by Apple Pay, card or cash.
- Use wellness language only; never make medical claims; flag anything that warrants a conversation with a doctor to the General Manager.
- Record session notes in the booking system and explain memberships (Single Reset, Restore, Elevate) accurately.

## 4. Healer

**Purpose.** Open every guest's journey with care. Every first visit starts with a consultation with one of our Healers, who identifies the core problem and designs a personalised package or programme to regulate the nervous system and support energy healing.

**Reports to:** General Manager. **Works closely with:** Hosts and the Breathwork & Sound Healing Facilitator.

### Key responsibilities
- Lead the first-visit Healer consultation ([30–45 min proposed]) with every new guest: listen, review the Health Questionnaire (AUR-FRM-02) and identify the core issue behind how the guest feels.
- Design a personalised package or programme from the launch modalities, Guided Breathwork and Sound Healing, respecting every contraindication in AUR-FRM-03.
- Hand the package to the Hosts on shift with clear notes, so they can suggest the right combinations visit by visit.
- Hold follow-up and progress reviews with members, including the Elevate quarterly guided protocol review.
- Bring energy-healing and holistic practice into the studio in line with the Aura values: the nervous system first; whole person, always.
- Use wellness language only; never diagnose, treat or prescribe; flag anything that warrants a conversation with a doctor to the guest and the General Manager.
- Keep consultation notes accurate and confidential in the booking system, in line with the PDPL and AUR-SAF-03.
- Support launch-week, founding-member and community sessions with the Marketing & Community Executive.
