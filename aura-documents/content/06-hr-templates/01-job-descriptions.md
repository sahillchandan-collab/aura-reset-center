# Job Descriptions

> Document No. AUR-HR-01 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

> Draft for internal use. To be reviewed by a UAE-qualified legal adviser once the trade license is issued.

This document sets out the eight founding roles for the Dubai studio at 332 Jumeirah First, Jumeirah Beach Road, Jumeirah 1, opening November 2026. Each role exists to protect one promise: every guest is met by someone who knows the modalities and walks with them from arrival to the last exhale.

**Applies to all roles.** The studio is open seven days a week, [Opening hours] (planning assumption 8:00am–8:00pm). Shifts are rostered under the Staff rota template (AUR-HR-08) within the working-hours limits of UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021. All staff complete Elevé Health training relevant to their role after hire, current CPR/BLS certification, and the onboarding plan in AUR-HR-05. Salaries and benefits are confirmed in the individual offer letter (AUR-HR-03).

## 1. Lead Host / Operations Lead

**Purpose.** Lead the Host team on the studio floor and keep every shift running to the Elevé standard: safe equipment, calm guests, on-time sessions.

**Reports to:** General Manager. **Direct reports:** Hosts, Spa Attendants (shift supervision of Guest Relations Executives).

### Key responsibilities
- Lead the opening and closing checks for all 11 modalities, including cryotherapy chamber, cold plunge water temperature (5 °C), sauna and float tank readiness.
- Build and publish the weekly rota; ensure minimum staffing per shift and approve shift swaps.
- Host guests personally, especially first-timers booking "Not sure yet — build my reset", and model the Aura guest journey.
- Coach Hosts on protocols, contrast pairings and the opening breathing ritual: "In for four, out for six."
- Act as first point of escalation for guest concerns, incidents and near misses; log them in line with AUR-SAF-01 and AUR-SAF-05.
- Liaise with Elevé Health technicians on service visits, faults and protocol updates; keep the equipment log current.
- Track consumables (robes, towels, slippers, Epsom salt, salt cabin supplies) and raise orders.
- Lead the founding-member welcome and the November launch-week floor plan.

## 2. Host (Wellness Host)

**Purpose.** Be the guest's guide through their reset. A host, not a receptionist: you know the modalities and walk with each guest from arrival to the last exhale.

**Reports to:** Lead Host / Operations Lead.

### Key responsibilities
- Welcome each guest, check how they arrived (the nervous system first), and lead the opening breathing ritual.
- Confirm pre-session screening and consent forms are complete before every session.
- Operate Elevé equipment to protocol: E-CRYO PRO cryotherapy, red-light bed, infrared sauna, compression boots, dry float bed, E-Salt Cabin and the Polar Recovery cold plunge (always host-guided).
- Prepare and reset float tank and treatment rooms; support hyperbaric sessions under Clinical Lead supervision.
- Build 3-step sequences for first-timers and suggest contrast pairings (cold plunge with infrared sauna; compression with sauna or cryotherapy; salt cabin before float or breathwork).
- Use wellness language only; never make medical claims; flag anything that warrants a conversation with a doctor to the Clinical Lead.
- Record session notes in the booking system and explain memberships (Single Reset, Restore, Elevate) accurately.

|---|---|

| Screening and consent completed before session | 100% |
| Session protocol and time-limit compliance (e.g. cryotherapy 3 min) | 100% |
| Room reset within allocated changeover time | [Target]% |
| Membership or rebooking conversions | [Target] per month |

### Qualifications & licences
- Background in spa, wellness, fitness, sports therapy or premium hospitality. [Minimum experience]
- Current CPR/BLS certification.
- Elevé Health device training per modality after hire, with sign-off before operating any unit alone.
- Breathwork or bodywork qualifications an advantage.

### Skills
- Genuine interest in nervous-system regulation and recovery.
- Steady, reassuring presence; reads guest comfort and anxiety well.
- High hygiene standards; physically able to lift, clean and stand for long periods.
- English essential; additional languages an advantage.

- Greet guests, check them in, issue robes, towels and slippers, and hand over to the Host.

- Process payments and membership sign-ups, pauses and cancellations before the next billing date; manage guest passes and founding-member benefits.
- Handle enquiries and first-line complaints in line with AUR-SAF-05; escalate promptly.
- Protect guest personal data in line with the PDPL and AUR-SAF-03.

## 4. Clinical Lead (DHA-licensed Registered Nurse or GP)

**Purpose.** Provide licensed clinical oversight for IV Nutrient Therapy and hyperbaric oxygen sessions, and keep clinical practice safe and compliant, subject to DHA requirements.

**Reports to:** General Manager (operations); [Medical Director / DHA-registered responsible person] (clinical governance).

### Key responsibilities
- Administer IV nutrient therapy (energy, immunity, skin/glow, recovery, post-flight formulas) or supervise licensed clinicians who do, within the scope of the [DHA licence / approval reference].
- Carry out clinical screening and contraindication checks; decide whether a guest is suitable for IV or hyperbaric sessions.
- Supervise hyperbaric oxygen sessions (Elevé chamber, 1.3–2.0 ATA) and lead emergency response in the clinical area.
- Maintain clinical records, consent forms, medicine storage, cold chain and waste disposal to DHA and Dubai Municipality standards.
- Write and review clinical SOPs; brief Hosts on when to flag a guest for a conversation with their doctor.
- Lead CPR/BLS refreshers and emergency drills with the Lead Host.
- Prepare for DHA inspections and keep licences and certifications current.

| Clinical incidents and adverse events reported within 24 hours | 100% |

| Staff CPR/BLS currency | 100% |

### Qualifications & licences

## 5. Breathwork Facilitator

**Purpose.** Lead Guided Breathwork (45 minutes, 1:1 or small group) and anchor breathing practice across the studio.

**Reports to:** Lead Host / Operations Lead.

### Key responsibilities
- Design and deliver 1:1 and small-group breathwork sessions focused on downshifting from stress, sleep and focus.
- Screen guests for suitability and adapt or stop practice where a guest shows distress; escalate to the Clinical Lead where needed.
- Pair sessions with the salt cabin and float in line with Aura protocols.
- Train all staff in the opening ritual "In for four, out for six" and keep its delivery consistent.
- Contribute to community events, launch-week sessions and founding-member experiences with the Marketing & Community Executive.
