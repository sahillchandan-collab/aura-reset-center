# Recruitment Pack

> Document No. AUR-HR-02 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

> Draft for internal use. To be reviewed by a UAE-qualified legal adviser once the trade license is issued.

This pack guides every hire for The Aura Reset Center in Dubai, from requisition to first day. Use it with the Job descriptions (AUR-HR-01), Offer letter template (AUR-HR-03) and Onboarding checklist (AUR-HR-05). Hiring follows UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021: fair, non-discriminatory selection based on the role's requirements. Candidate data is handled under the PDPL and AUR-SAF-03 and kept only as long as needed.

## 2. Interview guide

**Format:** 45–60 minutes. Welcome the candidate as you would a guest: offer water, begin with one round of the breathing ritual ("In for four, out for six") if they are comfortable. Explain the studio, the Elevé Health partnership and the November 2026 opening. Ask the same core questions of every candidate for fairness; take notes against each one.

### Opening (5 minutes)
- Tell us what drew you to The Aura Reset Center.
- What do you know about nervous-system regulation, and why does it interest you?

### Calm under pressure
- Describe a time a busy shift went wrong. What did you do first, and how did you stay steady?
- Two guests arrive late, the cold plunge needs attention and a first-timer is anxious. Talk us through the next ten minutes.
- How do you reset yourself after a demanding day?

### Guest empathy
- A guest arrives visibly stressed and says they "just need it to work". How do you greet them?
- Tell us about a time you noticed something a guest had not said. What did you do?
- A guest wants to stay in the cryotherapy chamber longer than the 3-minute protocol. How do you respond?

### Nervous-system interest
- Which of our modalities would you try first, and why?
- How would you explain the idea of "the nervous system first" to a guest new to wellness?
- What wellness practices do you use yourself?

### Hygiene and safety standards
- Walk us through how you would reset a treatment room between guests.
- What would you do if you noticed the cold plunge water looked cloudy before a session?
- How do you handle a guest who has not completed their screening form?

### Role-specific questions
- **Lead Host:** How would you build a 7-day rota with minimum staffing on every shift?
- **Host:** Demonstrate how you would introduce the red-light bed to a first-timer.
- **Guest Relations:** A member wants to cancel. Show us how you would handle it.
- **Clinical Lead:** When would you decline an IV or hyperbaric session? How do you document that conversation?
- **Breathwork Facilitator:** Lead a 5-minute breathing practice for the panel.
- **Spa Attendant:** Which cleaning products would you avoid on which surfaces, and why?
- **Marketing & Community:** Write a two-line Instagram caption for the infrared sauna with no medical claims.
- **Admin & Accounts:** How do you track visa and licence expiry dates?

### Close (5 minutes)
- Availability for opening, mid and closing shifts, including weekends.
- Notice period, current visa status and expected start date.
- Candidate questions. Explain next steps and timeline.

> Do not ask about marital status, pregnancy, religion, nationality preferences or other personal matters unrelated to the role.
