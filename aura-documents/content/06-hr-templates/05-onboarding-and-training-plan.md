# Onboarding Checklist & Training Plan

> Document No. AUR-HR-05 · Version 0.2 (Draft) · Owner: Lead Host / Operations Lead · Effective: [Date]

This plan takes every new team member from offer acceptance to confident, signed-off practice in 90 days. The rule is simple: **no one operates an Elevé unit, or hosts a guest alone, until the training matrix shows sign-off for that modality.** Training records are kept in the employee file and reviewed at the probation review (AUR-HR-06).

## 5. Training matrix by role

Key: **R** = required before working unsupervised · **A** = awareness only · **–** = not required.

| Data protection under the PDPL (AUR-SAF-03) | R | R | R | R | R |
| Complaints handling (AUR-SAF-05) | R | A | R | A | A |
| Healer consultation and personalised packages | A | R (following packages) | A | R | A |

> Spa Attendant / Housekeeping completes as required: health & safety, emergency procedures and fire, hygiene and cleaning; and as awareness: breathing ritual, guest journey, CPR/BLS, data protection and complaints handling.

> Marketing & Community Executive and Admin & Accounts Assistant complete: breathing ritual, guest journey, health & safety, emergency procedures, CPR/BLS awareness, data protection and Elevé product orientation.
