# Staff Rota & Attendance Templates

> Document No. AUR-HR-08 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: Lead Host / Operations Lead · Effective: [Date]

The Aura Reset Center is open seven days a week, **9:00am–9:00pm**, aligned with the booking bands (Morning 9am–12pm, Midday 12–3pm, Afternoon 3–6pm, Evening 6–9pm). For now the floor runs with **2 Hosts per shift** across two shifts a day. Rotas must respect working-hour, rest-day and overtime rules under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 and the HR manual (AUR-POL-02).

## 1. Shift pattern

| Shift | Times | Covers booking bands | Main duties |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | 9:00am–3:30pm [confirm] | Morning, Midday | Opening checklist: equipment start-up [per Elevé protocol — pending], cryotherapy and cold plunge checks, sauna pre-heat, hyperbaric and dry float bed checks, laundry and linen par, refreshments set-up, WhatsApp and website leads, breathing ritual huddle |
| Closing | 2:30pm–9:00pm [confirm] | Afternoon, Evening | Evening hosting, retail, end-of-day cleaning and logs, laundry, equipment shut-down, end-of-day cash close (senior Host), closing checklist |

> The one-hour overlap (2:30–3:30pm) is used for handover, breaks cover, room resets and Elevé service visits. Whether opening checks need a start before 9:00am, and the closing finish after the last session, are [to be confirmed]. Breaks are staggered so no modality is left unattended. The end-of-day cash close is done by the senior Host on the closing shift and checked by the General Manager next morning [default — confirm].

## 5. Rota and swap rules

1. The rota is published every [Day] for the week starting [Number] days later. Check it at each shift.
1. Every employee has at least [Number] rest day(s) per week, as required by law and their contract.
1. Total hours, including overtime, stay within the limits of UAE labour law. Overtime needs prior approval on the Overtime authorisation form (AUR-HR-07).
1. Shift swaps need a completed Shift swap request (AUR-HR-07), signed by both employees and approved by the Lead Host **before** the shift. Unapproved swaps are treated as absence.
1. A swap is approved only if the covering employee is signed off on every modality booked for that shift and minimum staffing is still met.
1. Healer and facilitator sessions may be swapped only with another Healer or facilitator approved by the General Manager, and the guest is told in advance.
1. Sick leave: call or message the Lead Host at least [Number] hours before your shift where possible; a medical certificate is required as set out in the HR manual.
1. Clock in and out using [Attendance system]. Do not record time for another employee.
1. Leave requests around the November 2026 launch, Ramadan, Eid and other peak periods may be limited; apply early.
1. Rotas, timesheets and attendance records are retained for [Retention period] and handled under the PDPL (AUR-SAF-03).
