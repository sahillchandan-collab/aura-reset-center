## 1. Whole-Body Cryotherapy

### What it is

A 3-minute session in the Elevé E-CRYO PRO, an electric chamber that cools the air to as low as −110 °C. It uses no nitrogen. Touchscreen guidance and thermal-vision safety monitoring support every session, and your Host stays with you throughout.

### What you may feel

Intense cold on the skin, tingling, a rush of alertness and a warm glow afterwards. Many guests feel lighter and more focused.

### Risks

- cold-related skin irritation, redness or, rarely, frostbite or burns, especially on damp skin
- a temporary rise in blood pressure and heart rate
- dizziness, light-headedness or anxiety in the chamber
- allergic-type reactions in people with cold urticaria

## 2. Hyperbaric Oxygen

> Clinical modality. Subject to DHA requirements. Clinical Lead or DHA-licensed clinician sign-off is required before every session. Facility reference: [DHA licence / approval reference].

### What it is

A 60-minute session in an Elevé hyperbaric chamber at 1.3–2.0 ATA (atmospheres absolute), breathing concentrated oxygen while the air pressure around you is gently raised. Pressure is set by the clinician.

### What you may feel

Fullness in the ears as pressure changes, similar to flying. Warmth during pressurisation and coolness as pressure is released. Many guests rest or sleep.

### Risks

- ear or sinus discomfort or, rarely, injury to the eardrum (barotrauma)
- temporary changes in vision (usually after many sessions)
- claustrophobia or anxiety
- fatigue or light-headedness afterwards
- rarely, oxygen-related seizures or lung problems
- fire risk if prohibited items enter the chamber

### I confirm

| ☐ | Statement |
|---|---|
| ☐ | I have disclosed any lung, ear, sinus, heart, seizure or eye condition, and any implanted device. |
| ☐ | I am not pregnant and have no current cold, congestion or ear infection. |
| ☐ | I will bring nothing into the chamber except the approved clothing and items given to me. |
| ☐ | I have been shown how to equalise my ears and how to call the operator. |
| ☐ | I understand the session can be stopped and pressure released safely if I ask. |

## 4. Float & Sensory Reset

### What it is

A 60-minute float in a private tank of skin-temperature water with 500 kg of Epsom salt, so you float effortlessly in silence and darkness. You control the light and can open the door at any time.

### What you may feel

Weightlessness, deep rest and a loosening of muscle tension. Some guests notice vivid thoughts, emotions or a changed sense of time.

### Risks

- salt stinging in cuts or eyes
- light-headedness on standing up afterwards
- anxiety or disorientation in darkness or enclosed space
- slips on wet floors

### I confirm

| ☐ | Statement |
|---|---|
| ☐ | I have no open wounds, fresh tattoos, contagious skin condition or uncontrolled epilepsy. |
| ☐ | I am not under the influence of alcohol or drugs. |
| ☐ | I will shower before and after my float. |
| ☐ | I know how to use the light, door and emergency call button. |
| ☐ | I will stand up slowly and take care on wet surfaces. |

## 5. Cold Plunge

### What it is

A 2–5 minute immersion in the Elevé · Polar Recovery plunge, filtered water held at 5 °C. It is always Host-guided, and is often paired with the infrared sauna.

### What you may feel

A sharp gasp on entry, then intense cold, followed by calm, alertness and warmth as you warm up.

### Risks

- cold-shock response: gasping and rapid breathing on entry
- sudden rise in heart rate and blood pressure
- numbness, skin redness or, rarely, cold injury
- slips on entry or exit

### I confirm

| ☐ | Statement |
|---|---|
| ☐ | I am not pregnant and have no heart condition, uncontrolled high blood pressure, Raynaud's phenomenon or cold urticaria, or I have provided doctor's clearance. |
| ☐ | I have not had alcohol in the last 24 hours. |
| ☐ | I will enter only when my Host is present and follow their breathing guidance. |
| ☐ | I will keep my head above water and leave the plunge when asked. |

## 6. Infrared Sauna & Red-Light Bed

### What they are

**Infrared Sauna:** a 40-minute session in an Elevé full-spectrum infrared sauna room at 45–60 °C, warming the body directly for a deep sweat.

**Red-Light Bed:** a 20-minute session on an Elevé full-body light bed using 660 nm red and 850 nm near-infrared light.

### What you may feel

Sauna: deep warmth, heavy sweating and muscle release. Red-light bed: gentle warmth on the skin and a sense of calm. Most first-timers start with one of these two.

### Risks

- dehydration, dizziness, fainting or heat exhaustion (sauna)
- changes in blood pressure (sauna)
- skin redness or sensitivity, particularly with photosensitising medication (red light)
- eye strain if protective eyewear is not worn (red light)

### I confirm

|---|---|
| ☐ | I am not pregnant, or I have provided doctor's clearance. |
| ☐ | I have disclosed any heart condition, blood pressure issue, photosensitivity or photosensitising medication. |

| ☐ | I will wear the eyewear provided on the red-light bed. |
| ☐ | I will leave the sauna and tell my Host if I feel dizzy, nauseous or unwell. |

## 7. Salt Cabin

### What it is

A 30-minute session in the Elevé E-Salt Cabin, our flagship cabin. Dry salt air (halotherapy), red light, oxygen and aromatherapy combine in one sealed cabin. It pairs well before a float or breathwork.

### What you may feel

A light salty taste, easy breathing and quiet relaxation. Some guests cough briefly as airways clear.

### Risks

- coughing or throat irritation
- tightness of breath in people with asthma or COPD
- skin dryness or irritation
- reaction to aromatherapy oils
- discomfort in an enclosed space

### I confirm

|---|---|

| ☐ | I have no acute respiratory infection with fever. |
| ☐ | I will bring my inhaler into the cabin if I use one. |
| ☐ | I know how to call my Host and leave the cabin at any time. |
