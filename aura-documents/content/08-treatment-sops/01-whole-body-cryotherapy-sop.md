## 1. Purpose

This SOP sets out how a Host delivers a Whole-Body Cryotherapy session (Reset pillar, 3 min) in the Elevé E-CRYO PRO at the Jumeirah 1 studio. It keeps every guest safe, calm and accompanied from the robe to the last exhale. Cryotherapy is a wellness service. It is not a medical treatment, and Hosts make no medical claims.

## 2. Who may deliver

- Only a Host trained and signed off by Elevé Health on the E-CRYO PRO [training pending].
- The Host's sign-off is recorded in their training file before their first guest session.
- One Host stays with the chamber for the whole session. With 2 Hosts per shift, the second Host covers reception and the floor. Do not book cryotherapy at the same time as another session that needs a Host present throughout (Hyperbaric Oxygen, Cold Plunge).

## 4. Contraindications summary

Screen every guest, every visit, using AUR-FRM-03 section 1 and the universal checks. In short:

- **Do not proceed:** pregnancy; uncontrolled high blood pressure on the day; recent heart attack, unstable angina, arrhythmia, heart failure or pacemaker; Raynaud's, cold urticaria or cold allergy; DVT, peripheral vascular disease or stroke history; fever, infection or open wounds; severe claustrophobia.
- **Written clearance from the guest's own doctor needed:** controlled hypertension, cardiovascular history, diabetes with neuropathy, epilepsy, kidney disease, cancer, anaemia, hypothyroidism; guests under 18 (AUR-FRM-06).

There is no clinician on the floor at launch. If you are unsure, do not proceed. Escalate to the General Manager, who may refer to the [Medical Director named on the licence].

## 6. Guest preparation

- The guest changes in the changing room into dry cotton underwear or [Aura-branded cryo wear — confirm with Elevé protocol — pending], with the Aura robe and branded slippers over the top.
- Skin must be completely dry. No sweat, lotion, oil or heavy make-up. If the guest has just come from the sauna or a shower, they towel dry fully and wait [per Elevé protocol — pending].
- Remove jewellery, piercings, watches, glasses and contact lenses where possible. Metal held against skin can become very cold.
- Hydration: water before the session; no alcohol in the last 24 hours.
- First visit: the guest's Healer consultation and programme are on file, and the guest has had the guided walkthrough.
- Complete AUR-FRM-03 section 1, including the blood pressure reading.

## 7. Step-by-step delivery

1. Greet the guest by name and begin the breathing ritual together.
   *"Let's arrive first. In for four, out for six. How are you feeling today?"*
2. Confirm screening and consent (AUR-FRM-04; AUR-FRM-05 where applicable). Take and record blood pressure.
3. Walk the guest to the chamber. Show the door and how to step out.
   *"The door opens with a gentle push. You can step out at any moment. You're always in charge."*
4. Fit socks, gloves, ear band and mask. Check that everything is dry.
5. Set the session on the touchscreen [per Elevé protocol — pending]. First visit: lower setting and shorter time [per Elevé protocol — pending].
6. The guest removes the robe at the door and steps in. Take the robe and hold it ready.
7. Start the session and the timer. Stay in visual and verbal contact for the whole session.
   *"Keep moving gently. Breathe out slowly. Thirty seconds gone, you're doing beautifully."*
8. Count the time down calmly every [30 sec — per Elevé protocol — pending].
   *"One minute left. Long exhale. Nearly there."*
9. At the end, open the door, wrap the guest in the robe, and guide them out slowly.
   *"Welcome back. Notice the warmth returning. Take your time."*
10. Remove socks and gloves; check the skin of hands, feet and any area that felt very cold.

## 8. Monitoring and stop criteria

Watch the guest and the thermal-vision display throughout. Stop the session and bring the guest out at once if:

- the guest asks to stop, or seems frightened or panicked;
- the guest stops moving or stops answering clearly;
- the guest reports dizziness, chest discomfort, breathlessness, numbness or burning pain;
- skin looks white, waxy or blotchy, or the thermal display shows a warning;
- clothing, socks or gloves become damp;
- the unit shows any fault or alarm.

A Host may shorten or stop any session. Never extend beyond the time set in the Elevé protocol.

## 9. Post-session care, refreshments and suggested combinations

- Seat the guest in the lounge in robe and slippers. Offer a warm herbal tea or infused water [menu to confirm].
- Encourage gentle movement to rewarm. Do not send the guest straight into a hot sauna; any interval is [per Elevé protocol — pending].
- Check skin again after [5] minutes. Any redness that does not fade, blistering or numbness goes to section 12.
- Suggested combinations: cryotherapy pairs well with Compression Recovery. A Host may also suggest Guided Breathwork or Sound Healing to settle afterwards. Always follow the guest's Healer programme.
  *"Many guests love the boots after cryo. Would you like me to see if they're free?"*

## 11. If the unit faults

There is only one cryotherapy chamber. If it faults:

1. Stop the session and bring the guest out safely. Their care comes first.
2. Do not attempt repairs. Note any error code. Switch off [per Elevé protocol — pending].
3. Place an "Out of use" sign on the door and inform the General Manager.
4. Contact Elevé Health technical support [Phone] and record the call in the maintenance log.
5. Offer the guest a suitable alternative modality on the day (after screening), or rebook their cryotherapy. Cancel or move later cryo bookings the same day.
6. Follow AUR-SOP-09 for fault handling and guest service recovery.

## 12. Emergency actions

Follow AUR-SAF-04. In particular:

- Cold injury: section 4.2. Remove damp items, warm gently, do not rub, take the device out of use and inform Elevé.
- Faint or unwell: section 4.1. Lie the guest down, keep them warm.
- Call 998 for unresponsiveness, chest pain, breathing difficulty, seizure or confusion. Bring the AED from [AED location].
- Never leave the guest alone. Complete the Incident Report Form (AUR-SAF-04 section 7) before the end of the shift.
