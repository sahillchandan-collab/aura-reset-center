## 1. Purpose

Guided Breathwork is a 45-minute, guide-led Reset session, offered 1:1 or in a small group. It helps guests downshift from stress and supports sleep and focus. It uses slow, guided breathing to shift the nervous system towards calm. It is a wellness practice, not a therapy for any mental-health or medical condition.

## 2. Who may deliver

- A breathwork guide approved by the General Manager. This may be a Host or a Healer with breathwork training [training / certification — confirm].
- Group sessions: one guide for up to [__] guests. Group size is not yet confirmed.
- The guide must hold current first aid [certificate ref.].
- If the guide is one of the two Hosts on shift, the other Host covers the whole floor. Do not schedule cold plunge, cryotherapy or hyperbaric starts that need the guide during a group session (see AUR-TRT-11).

## 4. Contraindications summary

Screen every guest with AUR-FRM-03, section 11, and the universal checks.

- Do not proceed with intense techniques: recent heart attack or stroke; uncontrolled epilepsy.
- Medical clearance needed: pregnancy (no breath-holding or rapid techniques); high blood pressure; heart conditions; glaucoma; recent surgery; panic disorder, PTSD or a serious mental-health condition.
- No breath-holds for any guest with a cardiovascular or pregnancy flag.
- Never practised in or near water. Never immediately before entering the cold plunge.

For guests with a flag, use only slow, gentle techniques, such as the Aura ritual: in for four, out for six.

## 6. Guest preparation

1. Ask guests not to eat a heavy meal in the [___] hours before, if possible.
1. Ask about today's state, privately for group guests.
1. Explain that tingling, warmth or emotion can arise, and that this is common.
1. Make clear that every guest may return to normal breathing at any time.
1. Show where to lie or sit, and how to signal the guide quietly (a raised hand).

## 7. Step-by-step delivery

1. Welcome and settle. Begin with the Aura ritual.
   - *"Find a position that feels easy. Let's begin as we always do. In for four, out for six."*
1. Set the intention and permission.
   - *"There's no right way to do this. If anything feels like too much, simply breathe normally. You can't get this wrong."*
1. Body scan and arrival, about [___] min.
   - *"Feel the floor beneath you. Let your jaw soften. Let your shoulders fall away from your ears."*
1. Main practice, about [___] min, using approved techniques only.
   - *"Breathe in gently through the nose. Let the out-breath be longer than the in-breath."*
1. Watch every guest throughout. In a group, move quietly between mats. Check in with anyone who signals.
   - *"Just checking in. Would you like to slow down, or stay with this?"*
1. Integration and rest, about [___] min. Breathing returns to natural.
   - *"Let go of any pattern now. Let the breath breathe itself."*
1. Return slowly. Lights up gradually.
   - *"Begin to move your fingers and toes. Roll onto your side when you're ready. There's no rush."*
1. Close with one shared exhale, then sit quietly before standing.

## 8. Monitoring and stop criteria

Pause the technique for that guest, bring them back to normal breathing and stay with them if they:

- feel dizzy, faint, very light-headed, or have strong tingling or cramping in hands or face
- report chest pain, palpitations, headache or nausea
- become distressed, panicked or overwhelmed
- ask to stop

If symptoms do not settle within a few minutes of normal breathing, sit the guest up, offer water and follow section 11. Note any reaction on the session log.

If one guest needs the guide's full attention in a group, call the second Host to sit with them, or bring the whole group into a calm resting breath.

## 9. Post-session care, refreshments and suggested combinations

- Guests sit for a few minutes before standing. Help anyone light-headed.
- Offer water or herbal tea [menu to confirm].
- Ask one gentle question: *"What do you notice now?"*
- If strong emotion came up, offer quiet time in the lounge. Note it, with the guest's consent, for their Healer.

Suggested combinations (see AUR-TRT-11):

- Salt Cabin → Guided Breathwork, for calm (website pairing).
- Guided Breathwork → Dry Float Bed or Sound Healing, for sleep.
- Cold Plunge → full rewarm → Guided Breathwork (gentle only). Never breathwork immediately before the plunge.

## 10. Cleaning and reset

Follow AUR-SAF-02.

1. Bag used towels, blanket covers and eye-pillow covers for the in-house laundry.
1. Wipe mats and bolsters with the approved surface product [product — to be confirmed].
1. Reset the room for the next session and sign it off.

## 11. If equipment faults

There is no Elevé machine in this session. If the sound system, lighting, air-conditioning or heating fails, continue in silence if safe, or end the session calmly. Report the fault under the Equipment Fault & Out-of-Service Procedure (AUR-SOP-09) and tell the Senior Host.

## 12. Emergency actions

Follow AUR-SAF-04.

- Faint, dizzy or unwell: section 4.1.
- Chest pain, seizure, stroke signs or collapse: call 998 at once and follow section 4.11. Bring the AED from [AED location].
- Fire alarm: section 4.7. Guide guests out calmly; count everyone out.
- In a group, the guide stays with the person affected. The second Host calls 998 and looks after the rest of the group.

Complete an Incident Report Form (AUR-SAF-04, section 7) for every incident or near miss.
