## 1. Purpose

This SOP sets out how a Host delivers a Red-Light Bed session (Heal pillar, 20 min) on the Elevé full-body light bed. It is one of the sessions most first-timers start with, so it must feel calm, warm and unhurried. Red-light sessions are wellness services, not medical treatments. Hosts make no claims about skin, healing or any condition.

## 2. Who may deliver

- Only a Host trained and signed off by Elevé Health on the light bed [training pending].
- The guest is alone on the bed once settled, but the Host stays within call and checks in at the agreed points. The guest must always be able to reach the stop control and call the Host.

## 4. Contraindications summary

Screen every guest, every visit, using AUR-FRM-03 section 2 and the universal checks.

- **Do not proceed:** photosensitising medication (for example, isotretinoin in the last 6 months, some antibiotics); light-triggered epilepsy or photosensitivity disorders such as lupus or porphyria; active cancer or lesions in the treatment area.
- **Written clearance from the guest's own doctor needed:** pregnancy; eye conditions such as glaucoma or retinal disease; thyroid conditions; recent cosmetic laser or peel.

There is no clinician on the floor at launch. If unsure, do not proceed. Escalate to the General Manager, who may refer to the [Medical Director named on the licence].

## 6. Guest preparation

- Arrive with clean skin: no heavy make-up, lotion, oils or self-tan. Make-up remover and wipes are available in the changing room.
- What to wear: the guest may wear underwear, Aura-branded clothing, or go without, as they are comfortable; areas covered will receive less light. Robe and branded slippers to and from the room. [Confirm any clothing guidance per Elevé protocol — pending.]
- Remove jewellery, watches and glasses. Contact lenses [per Elevé protocol — pending].
- Hydration: a glass of water before the session.
- Confirm no photosensitising medication today. Complete AUR-FRM-03 section 2.
- First visit: the Healer consultation and programme are on file.

## 7. Step-by-step delivery

1. Greet the guest and begin the breathing ritual.
   *"Let's slow down together. In for four, out for six."*
2. Confirm screening, consent (AUR-FRM-04; AUR-FRM-05 where applicable) and medication.
3. Show the guest the room, the stop control and how to call you.
   *"This button pauses the lights. Press it any time and I'll be with you."*
4. Hand over the eyewear and explain why it stays on.
   *"These protect your eyes from the brightness. Keep them on the whole time."*
5. Leave the room while the guest disrobes and settles on the bed. Knock and wait for a reply before you return.
6. Check position and comfort, lower the canopy [per Elevé protocol — pending], and start the session.
   *"Twenty minutes, just for you. Let the warmth hold you."*
7. Check in at the agreed point [per Elevé protocol — pending], through the door, without entering unless asked.
   *"Just checking in. All comfortable?"*
8. When the session ends, the bed stops automatically [confirm per Elevé]. Knock, wait, and invite the guest to sit up slowly.
   *"That's your session complete. Sit up in your own time."*
9. Collect the eyewear. Leave the guest to dress or robe up in privacy.

## 8. Monitoring and stop criteria

Stop the session and check on the guest if:

- the guest asks to stop or presses the stop control;
- the guest feels too hot, dizzy, faint, nauseous or has a headache;
- the guest reports discomfort in the eyes, or the eyewear has come off;
- the guest reports skin burning, itching or unusual redness;
- the guest feels anxious or closed in under the canopy;
- the unit shows a fault, overheats or any LEDs flicker or fail.

Never extend beyond the time in the Elevé protocol, and do not book back-to-back sessions for the same guest outside the protocol interval.

## 9. Post-session care, refreshments and suggested combinations

- Offer water or a herbal tea [menu to confirm] in the lounge.
- Ask how the guest feels and note it.
  *"How does your body feel now, compared with when you arrived?"*
- The guest may apply their own moisturiser after the session.
- Suggested combinations: red light is a gentle start for first-timers and sits well with the Infrared Sauna, the Dry Float Bed, Guided Breathwork or Sound Healing. Order and intervals [per Elevé protocol — pending]. Always follow the guest's Healer programme.

## 11. If the unit faults

There is only one light bed. If it faults:

1. Stop the session and help the guest up. Make sure they are comfortable.
2. Do not attempt repairs. Note any error code and switch off [per Elevé protocol — pending].
3. Place an "Out of use" sign and inform the General Manager.
4. Call Elevé Health technical support [Phone]; record in the maintenance log.
5. Offer a suitable alternative (for example, Infrared Sauna or Dry Float Bed, after screening) or rebook. Move later red-light bookings that day.
6. Follow AUR-SOP-09 for fault handling and guest service recovery.

## 12. Emergency actions

Follow AUR-SAF-04:

- Faint, dizzy or unwell: section 4.1.
- Skin burn or heat injury: section 4.3.
- Seizure, unresponsiveness or breathing difficulty: call 998, bring the AED from [AED location], and stay with the guest.
- Take the device out of use if it may be involved. Complete the Incident Report Form (AUR-SAF-04 section 7) before the end of the shift.
