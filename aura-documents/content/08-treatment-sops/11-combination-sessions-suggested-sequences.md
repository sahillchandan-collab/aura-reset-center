## 1. Purpose

Guests often ask, *"What should I do together?"* This SOP sets out how Hosts suggest, book and run combinations safely, using only the services offered at launch. It turns the seven goals in our "Find your reset" picker into simple, repeatable sequences.

Launch services that may be combined: Whole-Body Cryotherapy, Red-Light Bed, Infrared Sauna, Hyperbaric Oxygen, Dry Float Bed, Salt Cabin, Cold Plunge, Compression Recovery, Guided Breathwork and Sound Healing.

Not available and never suggested: IV Nutrient Therapy (a possible future service only) and any wet float tank (there is none). No medical treatments are offered at launch.

## 2. Who may suggest and run combinations

- **Healers** design each guest's personalised programme at the first-visit consultation [30–45 min proposed]. Combinations follow that programme.
- **Hosts** suggest combinations within the programme and this SOP, and run each step under its own SOP (AUR-TRT-01 to AUR-TRT-10).
- A Host may always shorten, change or stop a combination. A Host never adds a step the Healer programme or screening rules out.
- Questions go to the Senior Host on shift, then the General Manager, who may consult the [Medical Director named on the licence].

## 3. How Hosts suggest a combination

1. Start from the guest's state, not the menu. Use the breathing ritual: in for four, out for six.
   - *"How are you arriving today? What would you like to leave with?"*
1. Match their answer to one of the seven goals (section 6).
1. Check screening for **every** step in the sequence (AUR-FRM-03), not only the first.
1. Offer one clear suggestion and one gentler option. Never push.
   - *"For sleep, many guests enjoy the red-light bed, then the dry float bed, then a sound session to close. If you'd prefer something shorter, the red light and float alone are lovely."*
1. Confirm time available, the total price AED [___] and how it is paid (membership session, Single Reset with add-on, or package).
1. Record the agreed plan on the Combination Plan (section 9) before the first step starts.

First-timers who choose "Not sure yet — build my reset" receive a 3-step sequence after their Healer consultation. Most first-timers start with the Infrared Sauna or the Red-Light Bed.

## 8. Scheduling with one unit of each machine and two Hosts

Every machine is a single unit, and there are two Hosts per shift. Plan the whole sequence before the guest arrives.

1. **Book the whole chain as linked bookings** in [Booking system — to be chosen], including turnaround cleaning time for each unit [___] min. A unit is not released until the Host signs it off (AUR-SAF-02).
1. **Host-present sessions need one Host for the whole session.** These are Cold Plunge, Whole-Body Cryotherapy and Hyperbaric Oxygen. Never schedule two of them to run at the same time.
1. **Hyperbaric Oxygen holds a Host for 60 min.** During that hour, the second Host covers reception, WhatsApp, payments and every other guest. Do not book Cold Plunge or Cryotherapy in that hour. Do not run Hyperbaric Oxygen if only one Host is on the floor.
1. **Group Breathwork and Sound Healing:** if the guide or facilitator is one of the two Hosts, the other Host must be free to respond. Do not book Host-present sessions during a group session unless a Healer or third person is facilitating [confirm].
1. **Stagger starts** by at least [___] min so one Host is never needed in two rooms at once.
1. **Self-running sessions** (Red-Light Bed, Infrared Sauna, Dry Float Bed, Salt Cabin, Compression Recovery) still need a start, scheduled check-ins and an end. Plan these around the Host-present sessions.
1. **Shift handover** [14:30–15:30, confirm]: avoid starting a long sequence in the overlap without telling the incoming Host. Hand over every guest mid-sequence in person.
1. **Last start times:** the final step of a sequence must end, including cleaning, before close at 9:00pm. Last sequence start: [___].
1. If one unit faults mid-sequence, follow AUR-SOP-09 and offer the gentler option from section 6 or a rebooking under AUR-CLI-03.

## 10. Emergency actions and records

- Each step follows the emergency section of its own SOP and the Emergency & Incident Response Procedure (AUR-SAF-04). If a guest becomes unwell at any step, the combination ends.
- Keep the Combination Plan with the guest's screening checklists (AUR-FRM-03) and session logs in the guest file [Booking system — to be chosen].
- Share outcomes with the guest's Healer to refine the programme, with consent, under the Data Protection & Confidentiality Policy (AUR-SAF-03).
- The General Manager reviews combination records [monthly] and updates this SOP when Elevé protocols are received.
