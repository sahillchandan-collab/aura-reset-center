## 1. Purpose

This SOP sets out how a Host delivers an Infrared Sauna session (Heal pillar, 40 min) in the Elevé full-spectrum infrared sauna room. Many first-timers begin here. The aim is a deep, steady sweat in a calm space, with the Host checking in throughout. It is a wellness service, not a medical treatment.

## 2. Who may deliver

- Only a Host trained and signed off by Elevé Health on the infrared sauna [training pending].
- The guest is in the sauna alone or with [companions per Elevé protocol and booking rules — pending]. The Host checks in at set intervals and stays within call for the whole session.

## 4. Contraindications summary

Screen every guest, every visit, using AUR-FRM-03 section 3 and the universal checks.

- **Do not proceed:** pregnancy; unstable cardiovascular disease, recent heart attack or severe aortic stenosis; alcohol in the last 24 hours; fever or acute infection; haemophilia or active bleeding.
- **Written clearance from the guest's own doctor needed:** high or low blood pressure; heart conditions or pacemaker; diabetes, kidney disease, multiple sclerosis or heat sensitivity; metal or silicone implants; recent surgery.

There is no clinician on the floor at launch. If unsure, do not proceed. Escalate to the General Manager, who may refer to the [Medical Director named on the licence].

## 6. Guest preparation

- What to wear: Aura-branded clothing suited to the sauna, swimwear or a branded towel wrap [confirm sauna clothing with Elevé protocol — pending]. Robe and branded slippers to and from the room.
- Remove jewellery, watches and metal accessories; metal can become hot.
- Remove heavy make-up and lotions if possible; they can block sweat.
- Hydration: a full glass of water before entry, and water in the cabin. Avoid a heavy meal just before.
- Complete AUR-FRM-03 section 3, including agreeing the order and rest interval if pairing with Cold Plunge or another modality.
- First visit: the Healer consultation and programme are on file.

## 7. Step-by-step delivery

1. Greet the guest and begin the breathing ritual.
   *"Let's land here first. In for four, out for six."*
2. Confirm screening and consent (AUR-FRM-04; AUR-FRM-05 where applicable).
3. Show the room, door, controls the guest may use [per Elevé protocol — pending] and how to call you.
   *"The door is never locked. If anything feels too much, simply step out and I'll come to you."*
4. Brief the signs to leave: dizziness, nausea, headache or a racing heart.
5. Confirm the temperature and time. First visit: lower end of range, shorter time [per Elevé protocol — pending].
   *"We'll start gently today. You can always build next time."*
6. Leave while the guest settles. Start the timer once they are in.
7. Check in at every agreed interval with a knock and a few words through the door.
   *"Just checking in. Sip some water. How are you feeling?"*
8. At the end, knock, wait, and invite the guest out slowly. Hand over the cool towel and robe.
   *"Take your time standing. Let your body adjust."*
9. Walk with the guest to the lounge or shower.

## 8. Monitoring and stop criteria

End the session and help the guest out if:

- the guest asks to stop or does not answer a check-in clearly (enter at once);
- the guest reports dizziness, faintness, nausea, headache, palpitations or chest discomfort;
- the guest looks very flushed, pale, confused or unsteady;
- the guest stops sweating while feeling very hot, or feels chilled;
- the temperature reads above 60 °C or the unit shows a fault.

Never extend beyond the time in the Elevé protocol.

## 9. Post-session care, refreshments and suggested combinations

- Offer water or chilled infused water and a cool space to rest [menu to confirm]. Encourage slow sips.
- The guest may shower before dressing; fresh branded towels are in the changing room.
- Do not let the guest leave or drive while unsteady.
- Suggested combinations: the classic contrast is Infrared Sauna with Cold Plunge. Compression Recovery and the Red-Light Bed also pair well. Order, intervals and number of rounds [per Elevé protocol — pending]. Always follow the guest's Healer programme.
  *"If you'd like to try contrast, the cold plunge is ready. We'll take it slowly and I'll be right beside you."*

## 11. If the unit faults

There is only one infrared sauna. If it faults:

1. Bring the guest out and make sure they are cool and comfortable.
2. Do not attempt repairs. Note any error code and switch off [per Elevé protocol — pending].
3. Place an "Out of use" sign and inform the General Manager.
4. Call Elevé Health technical support [Phone]; record in the maintenance log.
5. Offer a suitable alternative (for example, Red-Light Bed or Compression Recovery, after screening) or rebook. Move later sauna bookings that day.
6. Follow AUR-SOP-09 for fault handling and guest service recovery.

## 12. Emergency actions

Follow AUR-SAF-04:

- Faint, dizzy or overheated: section 4.1. Move to a cool area, lie flat with legs raised if faint, small sips of water.
- Burn from a hot surface: section 4.3. Cool running water for 20 minutes.
- Fire or smoke: section 4.7. Raise the alarm and call 997.
- Call 998 for unresponsiveness, chest pain, breathing difficulty, confusion or seizure. Bring the AED from [AED location].
- Complete the Incident Report Form (AUR-SAF-04 section 7) before the end of the shift.
