## 1. Purpose

This SOP sets out how a Host delivers a Hyperbaric Oxygen session (Heal pillar, 60 min) in the Elevé hyperbaric chamber. It is a wellness session, not a medical treatment. Hosts never describe it as treating any condition. There is no clinician on the floor at launch.

## 2. Who may deliver

- Only a Host trained and signed off by Elevé Health as a hyperbaric operator [training pending].
- **The Host remains with the chamber, at the control panel, for the entire session**, from pressurisation to the guest stepping out. The Host does not leave for any other task.
- With 2 Hosts per shift, the second Host covers everything else. Never run a session with only one Host on the floor, and never overlap it with Whole-Body Cryotherapy or Cold Plunge.
- AUR-FRM-03 section 4 requires senior Host sign-off before each session. Whether clinician sign-off is also required is [to be confirmed with DHA and the Medical Director named on the licence]. If it cannot be met, the session does not go ahead.

## 4. Contraindications summary

Screen every guest, every visit, using AUR-FRM-03 section 4 and the universal checks.

- **Do not proceed:** untreated pneumothorax; certain chemotherapy agents; pregnancy; acute cold, sinus congestion, ear infection or perforated eardrum; implanted devices not rated for pressure.
- **Written clearance from the guest's own doctor needed:** COPD, emphysema, asthma or chest surgery; epilepsy or seizure history; uncontrolled fever; heart failure, uncontrolled high blood pressure, diabetes on insulin; claustrophobia; recent ear or sinus surgery; eye conditions.

If unsure, do not proceed. Escalate to the General Manager, who may refer to the [Medical Director named on the licence].

## 6. Guest preparation

- What to wear: 100% cotton Aura-branded clothing only [confirm fabric per Elevé protocol — pending]. No synthetic fabrics, wool or own clothing. Slippers stay outside the chamber [per Elevé protocol — pending].
- Nothing enters the chamber unless Elevé allows it. Leave in the locker: phones, watches, trackers, hearing aids [per Elevé protocol — pending], jewellery, hair clips, lighters and any battery-powered item.
- No make-up, lotion, oils, perfume, hair products or nail varnish [per Elevé protocol — pending]. Wipes are available in the changing room.
- Water and a toilet visit before entry.
- Teach the ear-equalising technique (swallow, yawn or gently pinch the nose and blow) and practise it together.
- Complete AUR-FRM-03 section 4. First visit: the Healer consultation and programme are on file.

## 7. Step-by-step delivery

1. Greet the guest and begin the breathing ritual.
   *"Let's settle first. In for four, out for six."*
2. Confirm screening, consent (AUR-FRM-04; AUR-FRM-05) and the sign-off requirement.
3. Check clothing and pockets with the guest for prohibited items.
   *"Just one more look together. Nothing comes in with you except you."*
4. Explain the sensations: fullness in the ears, a little warmth as pressure rises.
   *"Your ears may feel full, like in an aeroplane. Swallow or yawn and they'll clear. Tell me any time."*
5. Help the guest into the chamber and get them comfortable. Close and secure the chamber [per Elevé protocol — pending].
6. Test the intercom once more. Begin pressurisation slowly [per Elevé protocol — pending], talking the guest through it.
   *"We're starting now. Keep swallowing gently. You're doing well."*
7. Once at session pressure, start the timer. Stay at the panel. Check in at least every [___] min [per Elevé protocol — pending].
   *"Halfway there. How are your ears? How's your breathing?"*
8. Decompress slowly [per Elevé protocol — pending], coaching the guest to breathe normally and never hold their breath.
   *"We're coming back to normal pressure now. Just breathe easily."*
9. Open the chamber only when the gauge reads ambient. Help the guest out slowly and sit them down.

## 8. Monitoring and stop criteria

Stop pressurisation, hold or decompress [per Elevé protocol — pending], and keep talking to the guest if:

- the guest asks to stop, feels anxious or closed in, or ear or sinus pain does not clear;
- the guest reports breathing difficulty, chest pain, tingling, visual changes, nausea or unusual sensations;
- the guest stops answering clearly, or the intercom fails;
- the panel shows any alarm or pressure reading you do not expect;
- there is any smell of burning, smoke, or fire alarm in the building.

Never open the chamber before it reaches ambient pressure unless the Elevé emergency protocol says so.

## 9. Post-session care, refreshments and suggested combinations

- Seat the guest in the lounge for [___] min [per Elevé protocol — pending]. Offer water or herbal tea [menu to confirm].
  *"How do your ears feel now? Take a slow sip and rest."*
- Advise the guest not to fly or dive for [per Elevé protocol — pending] after the session.
- Suggested combinations: restful sessions such as Guided Breathwork, Sound Healing or the Dry Float Bed. No cold or heat pairing unless the Elevé protocol allows it [per Elevé protocol — pending]. Always follow the guest's Healer programme.

## 11. If the unit faults

There is only one hyperbaric chamber. If it faults:

1. Follow the Elevé fault procedure to return the chamber to ambient pressure safely [per Elevé protocol — pending]. Stay with the guest and keep talking.
2. Help the guest out once safe. Do not attempt repairs. Note any error code, place an "Out of use" sign and inform the General Manager.
3. Call Elevé Health technical support [Phone]; record in the maintenance log.
4. Offer a suitable alternative after screening, or rebook. Move all later hyperbaric bookings that day.
5. Follow AUR-SOP-09 for fault handling and guest service recovery.

## 12. Emergency actions

Follow AUR-SAF-04 section 4.4 and the Elevé emergency protocol [Elevé HBOT emergency protocol ref.].

- Tell the guest calmly you are bringing them back to normal pressure. Decompress per Elevé protocol.
- The second Host calls 998 if needed, brings the AED from [AED location] and meets the ambulance.
- Fire or smoke: stop the oxygen supply per Elevé protocol, raise the alarm, call 997, decompress and evacuate per Elevé protocol (AUR-SAF-04 section 4.7). No water on electrical equipment.
- Take the chamber out of use, inform Elevé Health and the General Manager, and complete the Incident Report Form (AUR-SAF-04 section 7).
