## 1. Purpose

The dry float bed gives the guest the feeling of floating without getting wet. The guest lies fully clothed on a warm membrane over heated water for 30 minutes. It supports zero-gravity rest and a parasympathetic, "rest and digest" state. It is a Reset pillar session and one of the gentlest ways to close a visit.

There is no wet float tank at The Aura Reset Center. If a guest asks for "the float", this is it.

## 2. Who may deliver

- A Host trained and signed off by Elevé Health on the dry float bed [Elevé training record ref.].
- First visits only after the guest's Healer consultation, which sets the guest's programme.
- Trainee Hosts deliver only alongside a signed-off Host.

## 4. Contraindications summary

Screen every guest with the Contraindications & Host Screening Checklist (AUR-FRM-03, section 8) and the universal checks.

- Do not proceed: acute illness or fever.
- Medical clearance needed: pregnancy (later stages), heat sensitivity, recent back or abdominal surgery.
- Also ask about anything sharp or bulky in pockets, and any difficulty lying flat or getting up.

## 6. Guest preparation

1. Confirm the guest has changed into Aura-branded clothing and emptied their pockets.
1. Ask them to remove belts, jewellery that could press or snag, and glasses if they wish.
1. Offer the toilet before the session. Thirty minutes is longer than it sounds when you are lying still.
1. Explain how to signal or stop, and that you will be close by.

## 7. Step-by-step delivery

1. Greet the guest at the door and begin the breathing ritual together.
   - *"Before we start, let's breathe together. In for four, out for six."*
1. Check today's state and any changes since the Healer consultation or last visit.
   - *"How are you arriving today? Anything new in your body since we last saw you?"*
1. Help the guest lie down and settle. Place the pillow and blanket.
   - *"Lie back and let the bed take your weight. There's nothing to hold up now."*
1. Show the stop control or call button and confirm they can reach it.
   - *"If you'd like to stop at any point, press here, or just say my name. I'll come straight in."*
1. Start the session at the agreed settings [per Elevé protocol — pending]. Note the start time.
1. Dim the lights and leave quietly, or stay nearby if the guest prefers.
   - *"I'll be just outside. Enjoy the next thirty minutes. They're yours."*
1. Check in at [___] min by looking or listening, without waking a sleeping guest unless needed.
1. At the end, raise the lights slowly. Wait before speaking.
   - *"Take your time. Wiggle your fingers and toes. There's no rush to sit up."*
1. Help the guest roll to their side and sit up slowly. Stay with them as they stand.

## 8. Monitoring and stop criteria

Stop the session, help the guest up slowly and offer water if they:

- ask to stop, or feel anxious or closed in
- feel too warm, faint, dizzy, nauseous or unwell
- report pain, numbness or tingling that does not ease when they shift position
- notice dampness, a smell of burning, or any unusual noise or heat from the unit

If the membrane feels wet or the display shows a fault, stop at once and go to section 11.

## 9. Post-session care, refreshments and suggested combinations

- Guests may feel heavy or drowsy. Let them sit for a few minutes before walking.
- Offer water or herbal tea from the refreshments menu [menu to confirm].
- Ask one gentle question and note the answer: *"What's different now, compared with when you came in?"*

Suggested combinations (see AUR-TRT-11 for full rules):

- Red-Light Bed → Dry Float Bed, for sleep and deep rest.
- Salt Cabin → Dry Float Bed, for calm.
- Compression Recovery → Dry Float Bed, after travel.
- Dry float is usually best near the end of a visit, before a closing sound healing session.

## 10. Cleaning and reset

Follow the Cleaning & Sanitation Procedures (AUR-SAF-02, section 6.6).

1. Remove used pillow cover and blanket and bag them for the in-house laundry.
1. Wipe membrane and frame with the Elevé-approved disinfectant [per Elevé protocol — pending]. Leave for full contact time. Never use sharp tools or abrasive pads on the membrane.
1. Check the membrane for wear or leaks.
1. Place fresh linen, reset lighting and sound.
1. Sign the room off before the next guest.

## 11. If the unit faults

1. Stop the session safely and help the guest up.
1. Do not attempt repairs or open any panel.
1. Switch the unit off as trained [per Elevé protocol — pending].
1. Place an "Out of service" sign on the unit.
1. Follow the Equipment Fault & Out-of-Service Procedure (AUR-SOP-09): tell the Senior Host and General Manager, log the fault and contact Elevé support [Elevé support contact].
1. Offer the guest an alternative session or a rebooking, as set out in the Refund, Rescheduling & Service Recovery Policy (AUR-CLI-03).

A leaking membrane is also a slip and electrical risk. Keep guests away and dry the floor.

## 12. Emergency actions

Follow the Emergency & Incident Response Procedure (AUR-SAF-04).

- Guest faint or unwell: section 4.1. Lie them flat with legs raised if they feel faint.
- Power failure: section 4.8. Open or lift any canopy and help the guest up slowly.
- Medical emergency: section 4.11. Call 998 and bring the AED from [AED location].
- Fire: section 4.7.

Complete an Incident Report Form (AUR-SAF-04, section 7) for every incident or near miss.
