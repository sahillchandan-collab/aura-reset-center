# Brand Guidelines (Written Standards)

> Document No. AUR-GOV-06 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: Founder & CEO · Effective: [Date]

> These guidelines apply to everything that carries the name The Aura Reset Center: signage, stationery, digital, social, print, merchandise, partner materials and team communications. Approval of any exception sits with the Founder & CEO (see AUR-GOV-02).

## 1. Brand essentials

| Element | Standard |
|---|---|
| Brand name | **The Aura Reset Center** (always in full on first mention; "The Aura" thereafter) |
| Tagline | Nervous System Regulation |
| Pillars | Reset · Heal · Elevate (with middle dots, always in this order) |
| Descriptor | A recovery studio for the modern nervous system |
| Signature motif | The glowing amber aura ring |

> "Aura Wellness Center" is an internal nickname only. It never appears on anything a guest, partner or authority will see.

The brand name keeps American spelling "Center" in all cases. Body copy uses British/UAE spelling (colour, centre, programme).

## 2. Logo

### 2.1 The lockup

The logo is a stacked lockup of four lines, centred:

1. THE
2. AURA
3. RESET CENTER
4. NERVOUS SYSTEM REGULATION

"AURA" is the dominant line. All lines are set in Marcellus capitals. The tagline line is the smallest and is letter-spaced. The aura ring may sit behind or around "AURA" in the primary version.

### 2.2 Versions

| Version | Use |
|---|---|
| Primary: Espresso lockup with Amber ring on Cream or Shell | Default for print, stationery, signage |
| Reversed: Cream lockup with Amber ring on Espresso | Dark backgrounds, night signage, social |
| Mono Espresso | Single-colour print, embossing, documents |
| Mono Cream | Single-colour on dark or photographic backgrounds |
| Short mark: AURA with ring | Social avatars, favicons, small merchandise, where the full lockup would fall below minimum size |

### 2.3 Clear space

Keep a clear space around the lockup equal to the height of the letter "A" in "AURA" on all four sides. No text, edges, images or other logos may enter this space. On signage the clear space is doubled.

### 2.4 Minimum size

| Medium | Full lockup | Short mark |
|---|---|---|
| Print | [__] mm wide (tagline line must remain legible) | [__] mm wide |
| Digital | [__] px wide | [__] px wide |

If the tagline line becomes unreadable, use the lockup without the tagline or the short mark.

### 2.5 Logo don'ts

- Do not stretch, squash, rotate or skew the lockup.
- Do not re-arrange the four lines or set them on one line.
- Do not change the typeface or set the logo in lower case.
- Do not recolour outside the approved palette or add gradients other than the aura-ring glow.
- Do not add drop shadows, outlines, bevels or effects.
- Do not place the logo on busy photography without a calm, low-contrast area behind it.
- Do not separate "RESET CENTER" from "AURA".
- Do not combine the logo with another logo inside its clear space. Co-branding with Elevé Health follows section 2.6.

The Aura logo leads. Elevé Health appears as "Technology partner: Elevé Health" or with the Elevé logo at no more than [__]% of the height of The Aura lockup, separated by a thin Clay divider. Use of the Elevé name and logo follows the partner agreement and Elevé's own guidelines.

| Cocoa | #4A3A2C | Body text | Paragraphs, forms, documents |
| Taupe | #7A6753 | Muted text | Captions, metadata, secondary labels |
| Bronze | #A6754C | Primary accent | Buttons, key highlights, links, icons |
| Light bronze | #C79A6D | Secondary accent | Hover states, fine rules on dark backgrounds |
| Amber "aura light" | #F0A968 | Glow | The aura ring and its glow only; sparing highlights |
| Halo | #FFD3A0 | Glow edge | Outer glow of the ring, soft highlights |

| Sand | #E8D7C5 | Soft surface | Panels, table header fills |

**Colour rules**

- Backgrounds are warm and light: Cream or Shell. Espresso is the only dark background.
- Body text is Cocoa on light backgrounds; never Amber or Halo for text.
- Amber and Halo are reserved for light and glow. Use them as accents, not fills of large areas.
- Do not introduce cool greys, blues, greens or pure black and white.
- Check contrast for accessibility on all digital text; Taupe is for secondary text only.

## 4. Typography

| Typeface | Role | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Marcellus** | Display | Logo, headings, titles, signage. Flared Roman capitals; generous letter-spacing in caps |
| **Jost** | Body and UI | Paragraphs, forms, tables, buttons, menus. Thin geometric sans; use Light or Regular for body |
| **Caveat** | Accent script | Signatures only, for example a handwritten "Eutemia" sign-off or a Host's note card. Never for headings, body or prices |

### 4.1 Hierarchy

| Level | Typeface | Style | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 / page title | Marcellus | Caps or title case, letter-spaced | RESET · HEAL · ELEVATE |
| H2 / section | Marcellus | Title case | Four things we will not compromise on |
| H3 / sub-section | Jost | Medium, small caps or uppercase, letter-spaced | THE ELEVÉ STANDARD |
| Body | Jost | Light or Regular, comfortable line spacing | Every protocol starts by asking what state you arrived in. |
| Caption / metadata | Jost | Light, Taupe | 20 min · Heal |
| Button / label | Jost | Regular, uppercase, letter-spaced | REQUEST A TIME |
| Signature | Caveat | Regular, Bronze or Espresso | With care, Eutemia |

Fallbacks where the brand fonts are unavailable: a classic serif for Marcellus, a geometric sans for Jost. Keep text left-aligned for body copy; centre only titles, the logo and short statements.

## 5. The aura-ring motif

- The ring is a soft, glowing circle in Amber fading to Halo, suggesting breath, light and calm.
- Use one ring per composition. It is a focal point, not a pattern.
- It may frame the logo, a single word ("Reset"), or a calm area of a photograph.
- Keep the glow soft. No hard outlines, no rainbow effects, no animation faster than a slow breath (for example, a four-second in, six-second out pulse in digital use).
- Do not crop the ring so it reads as an arc unless the design is intentionally bleeding off the page edge.
- Do not use the ring to frame products for sale or prices.

## 6. Photography style

- **Light:** warm, natural or low amber light; glowing modalities (red-light bed, sauna, salt cabin) shown as calm light sources.
- **Mood:** stillness, the moment after; eyes closed, slow breathing, robes, towels and quiet spaces.
- **People:** real, varied guests in Dubai; Hosts shown alongside guests, guiding, never selling.
- **Equipment:** Elevé units shown clean, calibrated and in use with a Host present; no clinical clutter.
- **Composition:** generous negative space in Cream, Sand and Espresso tones.
- **Avoid:** before/after images, needles in close-up, extreme cold-shock expressions, gym-style intensity, stock images of other venues, and any image implying a medical result.
- IV therapy imagery shows the calm setting and the clinician, not the procedure.
- Model releases are required for every identifiable person, in line with UAE privacy and data protection law.

## 7. Tone of voice

Calm, warm, precise, unhurried. Short sentences. Second person ("you"). Sensory but never hype. No medical claims.

| Principle | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Calm | "Every visit begins with a breathing ritual. In for four, out for six." | "Get ready for the ultimate recovery blast!" |
| Warm | "Someone who knows the modalities walks with you from arrival to the last exhale." | "Our staff will process your booking." |
| Precise | "Filtered water held at 5 °C, always host-guided." | "Super cold water for amazing results." |
| Unhurried | "Arrive 10 minutes early, hydrated." | "Hurry, limited slots, book now!!!" |
| Second person | "Every protocol starts by asking what state you arrived in." | "Clients are assessed on arrival." |
| Honest | "Wellness services designed to support how you feel and function." | "Cures anxiety, insomnia and inflammation." |
| Sensory, not hype | "Silence and darkness. Skin-temperature water." | "Mind-blowing, life-changing float experience." |

## 8. Vocabulary

| Use | Instead of | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Host | Therapist, receptionist, attendant (for guest-facing guides) | Our people who guide guests are Hosts |
| Guest | Patient, client, customer | Members are also guests |
| Session | Treatment, procedure | "A 40-minute infrared sauna session" |
| Reset | Fix, cure | "Build my reset" |
| Modality | Machine, device, therapy (generic) | "Eleven modalities across three pillars" |
| Protocol | Programme of treatment | The sequence a Host tailors on the day |
| Studio | Clinic (in general marketing) | "Elevé Health's first partner clinic in the UAE" is permitted as the partner phrase |
| Supports, helps, may | Cures, treats, heals (as a medical claim), guarantees | Pillar name "Heal" is permitted as a brand term |
| Founding member | Early bird, VIP | First 100 Dubai members |
| Licensed clinician | Nurse/doctor (in marketing) | For IV therapy, subject to DHA requirements |

**Never write**

- Medical claims or diagnoses, such as "treats depression", "cures inflammation", "detox your organs".
- Guarantees of results or timeframes.
- Prices in any brand or marketing material until approved: use AED [___] in drafts.
- Comparisons that name competitors.

Always keep the medical position wording where health is discussed: "Our modalities are wellness services designed to support how you feel and function. They are not a substitute for medical care."
