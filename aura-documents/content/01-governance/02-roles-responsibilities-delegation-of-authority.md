## 1. Purpose and scope

This document sets out what each leader of The Aura Reset Center is responsible for, and who may decide, recommend or must be consulted on key business matters. It applies to [Registered Company Name] ([Trade License No.]) and to the Dubai flagship in Jumeirah 1. Where the split between the Founder & CEO and the CEO is not yet settled, it is marked **[To be agreed by the CEOs]**. Until agreed, any such decision requires the approval of both.

## 2. Responsibilities

### 2.1 Founder & Chief Executive Officer

- Holds the vision, values and brand of The Aura: "Reset · Heal · Elevate" and the four things we will not compromise on.
- Owns the guest experience philosophy and the modality menu, including any new modality or protocol.
- Owns the relationship with Elevé Health at partner level, including the partner-clinic agreement.
- Approves brand guidelines, the company profile and any public statement in the founder's name.
- Leads investor, landlord and strategic partner relationships with the CEO [To be agreed by the CEOs].
- Links the Dubai flagship with the United States location in Santa Ana, including membership reciprocity.
- Chairs the quarterly leadership review.

### 2.2 Chief Executive Officer

- Leads commercial strategy, the business plan, annual budget and funding [To be agreed by the CEOs].
- Owns financial controls, banking mandates, audit, VAT registration and corporate compliance.
- Oversees licensing: trade license, [Licensing Authority] approvals, DHA requirements (including [confirm licensing requirements for hyperbaric with DHA / licensing authority]), Dubai Municipality and Dubai Civil Defence requirements.
- Approves senior hires, employment terms and the organisation chart (with the Founder & CEO).
- Approves material contracts, leases and insurance.
- Holds the General Manager accountable for operating performance against agreed targets.

### 2.3 General Manager

- Runs day-to-day operations of the Dubai studio within the approved budget and policies.
- Builds and leads the team: Lead Host, Hosts, Guest Relations, Healers, cleaning support, Marketing & Community, and liaises with the [Medical Director named on the licence] on oversight and escalation.
- Sets the rota of 2 Hosts per shift across opening hours of 9:00am–9:00pm, 7 days a week.
- Delivers the opening programme for November 2026, including the launch event and founding-member onboarding.
- Manages daily contact with Elevé Health on installation, calibration, service schedules and Host training.
- Owns health, safety, hygiene and incident management on site; escalates per section 5.
- Manages suppliers, stock (robes, Aura-branded towels and clothing, hotel-style Aura-branded slippers, refreshments, retail stock, consumables), the in-house laundry and facilities.
- Checks the end-of-day cash close the next morning.
- Reports weekly to both CEOs on bookings, memberships, guest feedback, incidents and spend.

### 2.4 Lead Host / Operations Lead

- Leads each shift; makes sure every guest has a Host from arrival to the last exhale.
- Maintains protocols, checklists and Elevé training records for every Host.
- Approves small service recoveries on the floor within the limits below.
- As senior Host on the closing shift (or delegates to the senior Host), completes the end-of-day close for Apple Pay, card and cash takings.
- First point of escalation for any guest concern or incident.

### 2.5 Healers

- Run every guest's first-visit consultation ([30–45 min proposed]), identify the core problem and design a personalised package or programme to regulate the nervous system and support energy healing.
- Lead Sound Healing and Guided Breathwork sessions [confirm] for 1:1 or small groups [up to __ guests].
- Recommend packages within the approved price list; any discount follows section 4.2.
- Work in wellness language only; flag anything that warrants a conversation with the guest's doctor.

### 2.6 Medical oversight

- No medical treatments are offered at launch. IV Nutrient Therapy is not offered; it is a possible future service only, subject to DHA licensing and on-site clinicians.
- A doctor is named on the licence as the [Medical Director named on the licence]. The doctor is not on the floor and provides oversight and escalation only.
- Any Host, Healer or the General Manager may stop or decline a session where a guest's health raises concern. This cannot be overridden by any commercial decision.
- If clinical services are added later, a Clinical Lead would be appointed and this document updated.

## 5. Escalation path

1. Host to Lead Host, immediately.
2. Lead Host to General Manager, within [__] minutes for moderate and serious incidents.
3. General Manager to both CEOs ([CEO contact order to be confirmed]), within one hour for serious incidents; the [Medical Director named on the licence] is informed of any health-related incident.
4. CEOs decide on external communication, insurer notice and any report required by DHA, Dubai Municipality or Dubai Civil Defence.

## 6. General rules

- Authority may be exercised only within the approved budget and company policy.
- A person may not approve a matter in which they have a personal interest; it moves one level up.
- Splitting a transaction to stay under a threshold is not permitted.
- In the absence of a holder, authority passes one level up, never down, unless delegated in writing.
- All approvals above AED [___] are recorded in writing (email is acceptable) and kept by Finance / Admin.
