## About The Aura Reset Center

**Reset · Heal · Elevate.** The Aura Reset Center is a recovery studio for the modern nervous system.

We bring advanced wellness technology and holistic practice together in one beautiful space, where every experience is created with intention. Your first visit begins with a consultation with one of our Healers, who identify what you need and design a personalised programme to regulate your nervous system and support energy healing. Each visit begins with a simple breathing ritual, in for four, out for six, and is guided by a Host who knows every modality and walks with you from arrival to the last exhale.

The Aura began in Santa Ana, California. Our Dubai flagship opens in November 2026 on Jumeirah Beach Road, Jumeirah 1, with an official launch evening and founding members welcomed first.

### Four things we will not compromise on

1. **The nervous system first.** Every protocol starts by asking what state you arrived in.
2. **Clinical hardware only.** One supplier, Elevé Health, installed and serviced by their technicians.
3. **A host, not a receptionist.** Someone who knows the modalities walks with you, every visit.
4. **Whole person, always.** Body, mind, nervous system and spirit.

## Our founder

**Eutemia Stockman, Founder & Chief Executive Officer**

Eutemia's wellness journey began in 2014 with energy healing and the connection between body, mind and nervous system. It expanded into biohacking, frequency-based wellness, fascia work and advanced technology. The Aura is the result: a place where technology and holistic practice meet.

"When I came to Dubai, something immediately felt different. I felt at home."

Eutemia's hope is simple: that everyone who walks through our doors feels cared for, supported, and inspired to become the best version of themselves.

## Our modalities

Eight Elevé modalities, one unit of each, plus Guided Breathwork and Sound Healing sessions, across three pillars. Each is guided by a Host, who will suggest combinations that suit you.

| Modality | Pillar | Duration | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole-Body Cryotherapy | Reset | 3 min | Elevé E-CRYO PRO electric chamber to −110 °C, no nitrogen, with thermal-vision safety monitoring |
| Dry Float Bed | Reset | 30 min | Zero-gravity rest, fully clothed, on a warm membrane over heated water |
| Salt Cabin | Reset | 30 min | Elevé E-Salt Cabin: dry salt air, red light, oxygen and aromatherapy in one sealed cabin |
| Cold Plunge | Reset | 2–5 min | Elevé · Polar Recovery plunge at 5 °C, always host-guided |
| Guided Breathwork | Reset | 45 min | One-to-one or small group [up to __ guests], guide-led |
| Red-Light Bed | Heal | 20 min | Full-body red and near-infrared light, 660 / 850 nm |
| Infrared Sauna | Heal | 40 min | Full-spectrum infrared sauna room, 45–60 °C |
| Hyperbaric Oxygen | Heal | 60 min | Elevé hyperbaric chamber, 1.3–2.0 ATA; a non-medical wellness session [confirm licensing requirements for hyperbaric with DHA / licensing authority] |
| Compression Recovery | Heal | 30 min | Sequential pneumatic compression boots, 7 zones |

Guests choose by goal: sleep deeper, calm the stress, recover faster, more energy, skin and glow, sharper focus, or beat jet lag. Your Healer builds your first programme with you, and your Host suggests combinations on the day.

IV Nutrient Therapy is not offered at launch. It may be introduced in future, subject to DHA licensing.

> Our modalities are wellness services designed to support how you feel and function. They are not a substitute for medical care. We do not offer medical treatments, and we will always flag anything that warrants a conversation with your doctor.
