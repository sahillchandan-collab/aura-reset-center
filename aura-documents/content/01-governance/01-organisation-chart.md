## 1. Purpose

This document sets out who leads The Aura Reset Center, who reports to whom, and which roles are planned for opening. It is the reference for recruitment, onboarding, the Delegation of Authority (AUR-GOV-02) and day-to-day escalation. It is reviewed at least every six months, and whenever a role is added, removed or changes reporting line.
