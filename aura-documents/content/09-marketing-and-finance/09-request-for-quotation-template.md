## 4. What your quotation must include

1. Unit price and total in AED, with VAT at 5% shown separately.
2. Your trade licence and TRN.
3. Delivery lead time, delivery charges and minimum order quantities.
4. Payment terms.
5. Warranty, service or replacement terms.
6. Product specifications, safety data sheets and certificates (Dubai Municipality, DHA or MOHAP, as relevant).
7. For services handling personal data: a data processing summary in line with UAE PDPL.
8. Two client references, preferably in wellness, hospitality or clinical settings.
9. Any assumptions or exclusions.

## 5. Evaluation

Quotations will be evaluated on price, quality and specification fit, compliance, lead time and reliability, service and warranty, and fit with the guest experience. The lowest price will not necessarily be accepted. The Aura may accept all, part or none of any quotation, and may ask clarifying questions.

## 6. Conditions

- This RFQ is not an order. An order exists only when The Aura issues a signed purchase order (AUR-FIN-10).
- Costs of preparing a quotation are the supplier's own.
- Information in this RFQ is confidential and for quotation purposes only.
- Suppliers must declare any relationship with The Aura's staff or owners.
- Equipment that interfaces with Elevé Health units must be approved by Elevé before purchase.
