# Purchase Order Template and Purchasing Procedure

> Document No. AUR-FIN-10 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure makes sure every purchase at The Aura Reset Center is needed, approved, fairly sourced, received in good order and paid correctly. It applies to all spending on goods and services in Dubai, including pre-opening fit-out, launch costs and day-to-day consumables. Approval limits follow the Delegation of Authority (AUR-GOV-02); if the two documents ever differ, AUR-GOV-02 prevails.

|---|---|---|---|

| Emergency (safety, equipment failure) | Best available | General Manager, ratified by CEO within [2] working days | Retrospective |

Rules:

- Do not split purchases to stay below a threshold.
- Recurring contracts are approved on their annual value.
- No one approves a purchase they requested or from which they benefit.

## 4. Procedure

1. **Request.** Requester completes a purchase request: what, why, quantity, required date, budget line.
2. **Budget check.** General Manager confirms budget availability against the approved budget line.
3. **Quotes.** Obtain quotes per section 3, using the RFQ template (AUR-FIN-09). Record on the comparison sheet (AUR-FIN-08).
4. **Supplier check.** New supplier: verify trade licence, TRN and bank details (confirmed by phone call to a known number, never by email alone). Add to the supplier register.
5. **Approval.** Approver signs the PO within their limit.
6. **Issue PO.** PO numbered sequentially and sent to the supplier. No goods or services are ordered without a PO where one is required.
7. **Receive.** Receiver checks goods against the PO: quantity, condition, expiry dates (salt, chemicals, clinical supplies), safety data sheets. Signs delivery note. Clinical supplies are received and stored by the Clinical Lead.
8. **Invoice.** Supplier's tax invoice must quote the PO number and show TRN and VAT at 5%.
9. **Three-way match.** Finance matches PO, delivery note and invoice. Differences go back to the General Manager.
10. **Pay.** Payment on agreed terms, by bank transfer to verified details. Two-person authorisation for payments above AED [___].
11. **File.** PO, quotes, delivery note and invoice filed together for at least the period required by UAE tax law.

## 5. Controls

- Changes to supplier bank details are verified by phone with a known contact before any payment.
- Company cards are held by named individuals, with monthly reconciliation and receipts for every transaction.
- Gifts or hospitality from suppliers above AED [___] are declined or declared to the CEO.
- The General Manager reviews spend against budget monthly and reports to the CEO.
