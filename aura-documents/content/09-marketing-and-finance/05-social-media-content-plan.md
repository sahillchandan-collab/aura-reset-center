## 7. Rules

1. **No medical claims.** Never say "cures", "treats", "heals [condition]", "detox", "burns fat" or similar. Use website wellness language: "designed to support", "many guests find". When in doubt, quote: "Our modalities are wellness services designed to support how you feel and function. They are not a substitute for medical care."
2. **No IV or medical services.** IV therapy is not offered at launch; do not post about it. Hyperbaric oxygen is described as a wellness session only. No before/after claims. Follow DHA rules on health advertising [DHA advertising approval reference, if required].
3. **Consent for guests.** Never film or photograph a guest without signed media consent [cross-reference to media consent form]. No guests in treatment rooms in swimwear without explicit written consent. Blur other guests and screens.
4. **No prices.** Prices are not final; direct people to book or message us.
5. **Elevé marks.** Use Elevé names and claims only as approved by Elevé Health.
6. **Creators** must disclose paid or gifted content as UAE advertising rules require [UAE Media Council licence requirements to be confirmed].
7. **Replies.** Reply to comments within [4] working hours in brand voice; reply to ad leads and WhatsApp booking messages within [___] minutes during opening hours (9:00am–9:00pm). Move health questions to private messages and invite a conversation with a Host or Healer.
8. **Approval.** Marketing lead drafts; General Manager approves; Founder & CEO approves Founder content.
