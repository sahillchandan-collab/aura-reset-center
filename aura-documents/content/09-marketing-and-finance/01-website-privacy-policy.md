## 3. What we collect

### Through the booking request form

| Field | Why we ask |
|---|---|
| Name | To know who is visiting and address you properly |
| Phone / WhatsApp | To confirm or adjust your booking and contact you on the day |
| Email | To send your confirmation and calendar invite |
| Location (Dubai or United States) | To route your request to the right studio |
| Treatment / modality chosen (or "Not sure yet — build my reset") | To prepare your Host and the room |
| Preferred date and time band | To offer you a suitable slot |
| Notes | Anything you choose to tell us so your Host can prepare |

### Through WhatsApp and our advertising

- **WhatsApp:** if you message us at [WhatsApp] to ask a question or book, we receive your name as shown on WhatsApp, your number and the messages you send, and use them to reply and manage your booking.
- **Ad lead forms:** we advertise on Meta (Instagram and Facebook) and Google. If you fill in a lead form inside one of our ads, the platform passes us the details you enter (usually name, phone / WhatsApp and email) so a Host can contact you quickly about your booking.

### Through the founding-member list

Name, phone / WhatsApp, email, location and, if you choose, the modalities or goals you are interested in. No payment is taken when you join the list.

### Information you should not send through the website

Please do not use the notes field to share detailed medical history. If something about your health matters for your visit, your Host will ask about it privately at the studio, using our health screening form. If you do include health information in a note, we will treat it as sensitive personal data, use it only to prepare for your visit, and move it into your secure guest record.

### Collected automatically

- Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, referring page and time of visit.
- Cookie and similar technology data (see section 6).

## 10. Keeping it safe

We use access controls, encryption in transit, strong passwords and two-step login on our systems, and we train every Host in handling personal data. No website is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the UAE Data Office as the PDPL requires.

## 11. Your rights under the PDPL

You can ask us to:

- tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it, and give you a copy;
- correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
- delete your data, where we no longer need it or you withdraw consent;
- restrict or stop certain processing;
- transfer your data to another provider, where technically possible;
- stop sending you marketing, at any time, through the unsubscribe link or by replying "STOP" on WhatsApp;
- withdraw any consent you have given, without affecting what we did before.

To make a request, email [Email]. We will reply within [30] days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the UAE Data Office.
