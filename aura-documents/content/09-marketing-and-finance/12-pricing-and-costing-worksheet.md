**Method:**

1. Host time = (session length + set-up and turnaround) × Host cost per minute. Include the breathing ritual and walkthrough for first visits.
2. Equipment per session = (unit cost ÷ expected life in months + monthly Elevé service cost) ÷ expected sessions per month.
3. Overhead allocation = monthly fixed overhead ÷ total expected sessions across all modalities (or weighted by room time).
4. Price excl. VAT = total cost ÷ (1 − target margin).
5. Round to a clean, calm number. Check against the Dubai market and the experience delivered.

## 5. Membership pricing logic

| Membership | Inclusions (from website) | Cost driver | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single Reset | One visit, one modality, guided first-visit walkthrough | Single-session cost plus first-visit time | AED [___] |
| Restore (monthly) | 8 sessions of any core modality; priority booking; 1 guest pass; 10% off IV and add-ons | 8 × weighted average session cost + guest pass cost + discount cost | AED [___] / month |
| Elevate (monthly, unlimited) | Unlimited core sessions; quarterly protocol review; 2 guest passes; 15% off IV and add-ons; first access to opening-week slots | Expected average usage × weighted cost + review time + 2 guest passes + discount cost | AED [___] / month |
| Founding member | Launch price locked 12 months; 2 guest passes a month for first 3 months | Price lock cost over 12 months; guest-pass cost over 3 months | AED [___] (locked) |

**Logic:**

1. **Weighted average session cost** = sum of (cost of each core modality × expected share of member bookings). IV therapy is excluded from core modalities and priced separately.
2. **Restore** should cost a member noticeably less per session than eight Single Resets, while still above the weighted cost plus margin. Effective per-session price = AED [___].
3. **Elevate** is priced on expected usage of [___] sessions a month, with a break-even usage of [___] sessions. Monitor heavy users monthly; capacity is protected through booking windows, not price.
4. **Guest passes** are costed at full session cost and treated as marketing spend, tracked against conversion (AUR-MKT-07).
5. **US use:** members using the Santa Ana location are charged no extra; an internal transfer rate of AED [___] per visit applies [to be agreed].
6. **Pauses** pause billing; no sessions accrue.
