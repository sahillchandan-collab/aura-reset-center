> Document No. AUR-SOP-06 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

## 1. Purpose and scope

Refreshments are part of how guests arrive, rest and leave The Aura Reset Center. This SOP covers the refreshment station, the menu, food-safety basics, when we serve, allergens, use of copper mugs, cleaning and stock.

Hosts prepare and serve refreshments. Before serving any food or drink, every Host completes the basic food-safety training required by **Dubai Municipality** [confirm requirement, for example a Person in Charge or basic food hygiene certificate, and any permit needed to serve drinks to guests].

> We serve simple, non-alcoholic refreshments to guests. We do not cook. If the menu grows beyond drinks and pre-packed snacks, the General Manager confirms Dubai Municipality requirements first.

## 5. Food-safety basics

These follow general Dubai Municipality food-hygiene principles. The General Manager confirms full requirements before opening.

- **Handwashing:** before preparing drinks, after touching money, phones, laundry or cleaning, and after using the toilet.
- **Personal hygiene:** clean uniform, hair tied back, no strong fragrance, cuts covered with a blue plaster.
- **Illness:** a Host with vomiting, diarrhoea or fever does not handle refreshments and tells the senior Host.
- **Temperature:** fridge at 1–5 °C. Cold drinks and cut fruit kept chilled.
- **Dates:** every opened item labelled with the date opened. First in, first out. Throw away anything past its use-by date.
- **Water:** use only [filtered / bottled] water for drinks and ice.
- **Ice:** scooped with a clean scoop, never with a glass or hands. Scoop stored outside the ice.
- **Separation:** cleaning chemicals are never stored above or beside refreshments.

## 6. Allergens

- Keep a written allergen list for every item on the menu (including herbal blends, which may contain nuts, seeds, citrus or flowers).
- Ask every new guest about allergies at intake (AUR-FRM-01). The Host checks the guest profile before serving.
- When a guest tells you about an allergy, offer only items you are sure are safe. If in doubt, offer plain water.
- Never guess. Never remove an ingredient from a mixed product and serve it as safe.
- If a guest shows signs of an allergic reaction (swelling, rash, difficulty breathing), follow AUR-SAF-04 and call **998**.

## 8. Copper mugs

Copper mugs are part of the Aura ritual and are also sold in our retail range (AUR-SOP-07).

- Use copper mugs for **room-temperature or cool water and infused water** [confirm with the supplier whether the mugs are lined, and which drinks are suitable].
- Do **not** use unlined copper mugs for acidic drinks (citrus, vinegar-based, fruit juice) or for hot drinks, unless the supplier confirms the mug is lined and suitable.
- Hand-wash only in warm water with mild detergent. No dishwasher, no abrasive pads. Dry at once to prevent marks.
- Polish [weekly] with [approved copper cleaner or lemon and salt, then rinse well].
- Remove from service any mug with a dented rim, loose handle or worn lining.
