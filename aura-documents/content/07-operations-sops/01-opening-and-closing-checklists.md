# Opening, Closing & Shift Handover Checklists

> Document No. AUR-SOP-01 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP sets out what the Hosts do to open The Aura Reset Center each morning, hand over between shifts and close each night. It applies every day of the week at our Jumeirah 1 studio, 332 Jumeirah First, Jumeirah Beach Road.

The studio is open to guests **7 days a week, 9:00am to 9:00pm**. There are **2 Hosts on each shift**. One of them is the **senior Host** for the shift and signs off each checklist.

| Shift | Proposed times | Main checklist |
|---|---|---|
| Opening shift | 9:00am – 3:30pm [confirm]; Hosts arrive at [8:15am — confirm] | Opening checklist (section 3) |
| Overlap | 2:30pm – 3:30pm [confirm] | Shift handover checklist (section 4) |
| Closing shift | 2:30pm – 9:00pm [confirm]; Hosts stay until [9:45pm — confirm] | Closing checklist (section 5) |

> Every machine setting, warm-up time, session limit and cleaning product in this SOP is **[per Elevé protocol — pending]**. Elevé Health operating instructions have not yet been received. Update this SOP when they arrive.

## 2. How to use the checklists

- Print one copy of each checklist per day, or use the digital version in [Booking system — to be chosen / shared drive].
- Write the time and your initials against each line as you complete it. Do not tick ahead.
- Mark anything not done as **N** and write the reason in the notes row.
- A unit that fails its check is **not used**. Tag it out and follow the Equipment Care, Maintenance & Fault Log (AUR-SOP-09).
- The senior Host signs the bottom of each checklist. The General Manager reviews the previous day's sheets each morning.
- Completed sheets are kept for [12 months].
