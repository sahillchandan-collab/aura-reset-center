# Staffing, Shifts & Escalation SOP

> Document No. AUR-SOP-10 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP explains how The Aura Reset Center is staffed each day, who is in charge on each shift, how we keep the floor covered, how we schedule our single units and Healers, and who to call when something needs a decision. It applies to all Hosts and Healers.

Working hours, rest breaks and overtime follow UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 on labour relations, the Employee Handbook and the Staff Rota & Attendance template (AUR-HR-08).

## 4. Keeping the floor covered

With 2 Hosts per shift, cover needs planning.

- **At least one Host is on the floor at all times** while guests are in the studio. The front desk is never left empty while a guest is in a session without another Host in sight of the treatment area.
- **Cold plunge sessions are always Host-guided**, so the Host guiding the plunge cannot cover the desk. Schedule plunges when the second Host is free.
- **Breaks:** each Host takes their break [length per UAE labour law and contract — confirm] at a time agreed at the shift huddle, when no session needs two Hosts. The senior Host plans breaks in quieter booking gaps. Breaks are taken one Host at a time, never both.
- If a Host must leave the floor (illness, emergency), the other Host calls the General Manager to arrange cover. Do not start new sessions that need two people until cover arrives.
- During the overlap (2:30pm – 3:30pm), the closing Hosts take over the floor while the opening Hosts complete laundry, restocking and handover.

## 9. Shift huddle

A 5-minute huddle at the start of each shift (8:50am and 2:30pm) led by the senior Host:

1. Today's guests: first visits, members, special notes.
1. Units out of service or due for maintenance.
1. Healer consultation times.
1. Who covers which units, the inbox and breaks.
1. Any message from the General Manager.

## 10. Sickness and absence

- A Host who cannot work tells the General Manager by phone at least [__ hours] before the shift (AUR-HR-08 and the Employee Handbook).
- The General Manager arranges cover. If no cover is found, the General Manager decides which bookings can safely go ahead with one Host, and the senior Host contacts other guests to rebook.
- The studio never opens with fewer than [2] trained people on site.
