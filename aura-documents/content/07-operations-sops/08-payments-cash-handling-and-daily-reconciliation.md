# Payments, Cash Handling & Daily Reconciliation

> Document No. AUR-SOP-08 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

> Draft for internal use. To be reviewed by a UAE-qualified legal adviser once the trade license is issued.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This SOP sets out how Hosts take payment, handle cash, close the day and deal with refunds and discrepancies at The Aura Reset Center. It applies to every Host who uses the POS, and to the General Manager who checks the daily close.

Our website promise is "No charge until your visit." Single sessions are paid at the studio on the day. Members are billed monthly under the Membership Terms & Conditions (AUR-CLI-04).

| Method | Status | Notes |

- We do not accept cheques.

## 3. POS steps for a guest visit

POS: [POS / booking system — to be chosen].

1. Open the guest's visit in the POS. Check the services used today against the booking and the guest profile.
1. Add any extras (second modality, retail).
1. For members, record the session against the allowance. Apply any member discount allowed under AUR-CLI-04.
1. Apply any discount only within the limits in the Roles, Responsibilities & Delegation of Authority document (AUR-GOV-02), with a reason recorded.
1. Tell the guest the total in AED, including VAT once registered.
1. Take payment:
  - **Apple Pay / card:** guest taps or inserts; never take the guest's card or phone from their hand.
  - **Cash:** count the cash received out loud, place it on top of the drawer until change is given, count the change back to the guest, then put the notes away.
1. Offer a receipt (printed or by email).
1. Close the sale. Never leave a sale open.

## 4. VAT and invoices

- The Aura Reset Center will charge VAT at **5%** once it is registered with the Federal Tax Authority and its **TRN [TRN]** is issued.
- Until the TRN is issued, do not show VAT on receipts and do not describe a receipt as a "tax invoice".
- Once registered, every sale produces a tax invoice that meets UAE VAT requirements, in the format in Tax Invoice & Receipt Templates (AUR-FIN-11): [Registered Company Name], address, TRN, date, invoice number, description, amount before VAT, VAT amount and total.
- Corporate guests asking for a full tax invoice with their company name and TRN: issue from the POS or ask the General Manager.

## 6. End-of-day close

The **senior Host on the closing shift** does the end-of-day close after the last guest leaves. The **General Manager checks** it the next morning.

1. Run the end-of-day report from the POS.
1. Run the card-terminal settlement (batch) report.
1. Count the cash drawer. Remove the float (AUR-SOP-01 records it for the next morning).
1. Complete the daily close-out form (section 7).
1. Seal the cash takings in an envelope, signed and dated across the seal. Place it in the safe with the float.
1. Leave the form, POS report and card batch slip in [the close-out folder / safe] for the General Manager.
1. Report any difference above AED [___] to the General Manager by message the same night.

## 8. Morning check by the General Manager

1. Recount the sealed cash envelope and float.
1. Match the close-out form against the POS and card settlement reports, and the bank statement once funds arrive.
1. Review discounts, comps and refunds against AUR-GOV-02.
1. Sign the form. Investigate any difference (section 10).

If the General Manager is away, the check is done by [delegate in writing] (authority passes up, not down: AUR-GOV-02).

## 9. Refunds

Refunds follow the Refund, Rescheduling & Service Recovery Policy (AUR-CLI-03).

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who may approve | Refund up to AED [___]: senior Host with General Manager approval; above AED [___]: General Manager with CEO approval (AUR-GOV-02) |
| Method | Always to the original payment method. Cash refunds only for cash payments. Never to a different person or card |
| Record | Refund line in the POS with reason, approver and guest name; listed on the close-out form |
| Guest confirmation | In writing by email (AUR-CLI-03) |
| Equipment-fault refunds | See AUR-SOP-09 and AUR-CLI-03 |

Never refund to "fix" a cash difference. Never refund your own purchase.

## 10. Discrepancies

| Difference | Action |
|---|---|
| Under AED [___] | Record on the form with a reason; General Manager notes |
| AED [___] or more | Recount with a second Host; message the General Manager the same night; check the POS for unclosed or duplicate sales |
| Repeated differences or missing cash | General Manager investigates and reviews with the CEO; follow the HR disciplinary process if needed |

- Never top up or take from the drawer to make it balance.
- Common causes to check: a sale rung as card but paid in cash, a duplicate transaction, a refund not recorded, a wrong float.

## 11. Security and conduct

- Hosts never share POS log-ins. Each Host uses their own.
- Card terminal and POS devices are kept locked away at night (AUR-SOP-01).
- In a robbery, do not resist. Hand over cash, stay safe, then call **999** and follow AUR-SAF-04.
- Gifts or tips from guests follow the Conflict of Interest, Gifts & Anti-Bribery Policy (AUR-POL-05) [tip policy to be decided].
