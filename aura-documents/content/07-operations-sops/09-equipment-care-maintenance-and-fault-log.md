## 1. Purpose and scope

Every machine at The Aura Reset Center is supplied, installed, calibrated and serviced by **Elevé Health**. We are Elevé Health's first partner clinic in the UAE. We hold **one unit of each machine**. If a unit develops a fault, that modality is **unavailable** until Elevé clears it. Careful daily checks, quick fault reporting and calm rebooking protect our guests and our schedule.

This SOP covers user checks by Hosts, the fault procedure, rebooking affected guests and the maintenance and calibration log. Treatment-by-treatment operating steps are in the separate modality SOPs [references]. Cleaning between guests is in AUR-SAF-02.

## 4. Fault procedure: stop, tag out, notify

Use this procedure for any fault, error message, unusual noise, smell, leak, temperature outside range or guest report of something "not right".

1. **Stop.** If a guest is in the unit, end the session safely using the normal or emergency stop [per Elevé protocol — pending]. Help the guest out. Guest safety comes first; if the guest is unwell, follow the Emergency & Incident Response Procedure (AUR-SAF-04).
1. **Make safe.** Switch off and isolate the unit only if Elevé guidance allows [per Elevé protocol — pending]. Do not try to repair it.
1. **Tag out.** Hang the "OUT OF SERVICE — DO NOT USE" tag on the unit with the date, time, fault and your initials. Block the unit in [Booking system — to be chosen].
1. **Notify the General Manager** immediately by phone. If not reachable, follow the escalation chain in AUR-SOP-10.
1. **Contact Elevé Health** technical support: [Elevé support contact — pending]. Give the unit, serial number, error code, what happened and photos. Faults are reported to Elevé the same day (AUR-GOV-02).
1. **Log** the fault in the fault log (section 6).
1. **Rebook affected guests** (section 5).
1. **Return to service** only when an Elevé technician (or Elevé remote support, in writing) confirms the unit is safe. The General Manager removes the tag and signs the log.

> Only the General Manager may decide to take a unit out of service for longer than the day, or return it to service (AUR-GOV-02). Any Host may tag out a unit on safety grounds at any time.

## 5. Rebooking affected guests

Because we have one unit of each machine, a fault affects every guest booked on that unit until it is fixed.

1. Print or list every booking on the unit for the rest of the day and the next [2 days].
1. Contact each guest by WhatsApp or phone using the "unit unavailable" template (AUR-SOP-03), in time order.
1. Offer, in this order (AUR-CLI-03): a suitable alternative modality at the same time, a new time for the same modality, or a full refund of any prepaid amount.
1. Check the alternative against the guest's screening (AUR-FRM-03) and the Healer's plan.
1. For a session stopped part-way through because of a fault, follow AUR-CLI-03: replacement session plus [goodwill gesture], or full refund.
1. Members: the session is not counted against their allowance.
1. Record every guest contacted and the outcome in the fault log notes.

> **Script:** "I'm so sorry, [Name]. Our [modality] needs some attention from our technical team today. I'd love to offer you [alternative] at the same time, or I can move you to [time]. Which would you prefer?"
