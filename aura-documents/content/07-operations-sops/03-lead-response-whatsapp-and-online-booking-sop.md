## 1. Purpose

Most new guests of The Aura Reset Center will come from paid ad campaigns on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google. Guests book online through our website and on WhatsApp. A lead answered in minutes is far more likely to book than one answered in hours. This SOP sets response targets, message templates, tracking and the rules on marketing consent.

## 2. Response-time targets

| Channel | During opening hours (9:00am – 9:00pm, 7 days) | Outside opening hours |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp message from an ad ("click to WhatsApp") | First reply within **5 minutes** | Auto-reply at once; personal reply by **9:15am** |
| Meta / Instagram lead form | Reply within **5 minutes** (WhatsApp if consent given, otherwise call or email) | First thing next morning, by 9:30am |
| Instagram direct message | Within **15 minutes** | By 9:30am |
| Google ad call | Answer within 3 rings; if missed, call back within **10 minutes** | Voicemail [to be set up]; call back by 9:30am |
| Online booking via website | Confirmation sent automatically; Host personal welcome within **1 hour** | By 9:30am |
| Email enquiry | Within **2 hours** | By 11:00am |

- The **senior Host** on each shift owns the inbox. The second Host covers when the senior Host is with a guest.
- A guest in front of you always comes first. If both Hosts are with guests, send the "one moment" quick reply, then reply in full as soon as you are free.
- Check WhatsApp at least every 15 minutes, and at every handover (AUR-SOP-01).

## 3. Tone

- Calm, warm, precise and unhurried. Short sentences. Second person ("you").
- Use the guest's first name. Sign with your own first name: "— [Host name], The Aura".
- No hype, no pressure, no exclamation marks in a row, no emoji in templates (a single one may be used by a Host if the guest uses them first).
- **No medical claims.** Say "many guests find it helps them feel calmer", never "it cures" or "it treats".
- Never describe IV Nutrient Therapy or a float tank as available. IV therapy is not offered at launch.
- If a question is medical, say: "That's a great question for your doctor. Our modalities are wellness services and not a substitute for medical care."

## 5. Message templates

Templates sent outside a 24-hour customer conversation must be approved WhatsApp templates [to be submitted to Meta once the account is live].

**First reply to an ad lead**

> Hello [Name], thank you for your interest in The Aura Reset Center. I'm [Host name]. We're a recovery studio for the modern nervous system in Jumeirah 1. What would you love to feel more of right now: deeper sleep, less stress, more energy, faster recovery, or something else? I can suggest a first visit and find you a time.

**Booking confirmation**

Please arrive 10 minutes early, hydrated, and skip heavy make-up or lotions. We provide robes, towels, slippers and clothing. Your forms: [link]. No charge until your visit. **Reminder, 24 hours before**

> Hello [Name], a gentle reminder of your visit tomorrow at [Time]. Arrive 10 minutes early, hydrated. If anything changes, reply here and we'll help. — [Host name], The Aura

**Reschedule**

> Of course, [Name]. I can offer [Option 1] or [Option 2]. Which suits you better? Your original booking stays in place until you confirm.

If the change is inside the cancellation window, explain the terms in AUR-CLI-02 kindly and once.

**No-show follow-up (same day)**

> Hello [Name], we missed you today at [Time] and hope all is well. Would you like to find a new time? [Any charge as per our booking policy: details.] — [Host name]

**Founding-member reply**

> Thank you, [Name]. Our founding membership is open to the first 100 members in Dubai. It includes your launch price locked for 12 months, two guest passes every month for the first three months, an invitation to our November launch evening and first choice of opening-week sessions. No payment is taken to join the list. Shall I add you? I'll need your full name and email.

Check the founding-member count before you reply. If all 100 places are taken, say so and offer the standard memberships.

**After-hours auto-reply (9:00pm – 9:00am)**

> Thank you for your message. The Aura Reset Center is open every day from 9:00am to 9:00pm. A Host will reply to you first thing in the morning. You can also book online at any time: [link]. If this is a medical emergency, please call 998.

**Unit unavailable (equipment fault)**

> Hello [Name], we're sorry: our [modality] is unavailable on [Date] for maintenance. We'd love to offer you [alternative] at the same time, or a new time for [modality]. Which would you prefer? (See AUR-SOP-09.)

## 6. Online bookings

1. When an online booking arrives, check the unit timeline for clashes (AUR-SOP-10).

1. Send a personal welcome on WhatsApp (if consent) within 1 hour in opening hours.
1. Check forms are sent. Chase missing forms 24 hours before.
1. Update the lead tracking table: status "Booked".

## 8. Consent for marketing messages

Personal data is handled under the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on personal data protection (PDPL) and our Data Protection & Client Confidentiality Policy (AUR-SAF-03).

- **Service messages** (confirmations, reminders, reschedules, follow-up after a visit) are sent because the guest asked to book with us.
- **Marketing messages** (offers, events, newsletters, founding-member promotions) are sent **only** when the guest has opted in, for example by ticking the marketing box on the intake form (AUR-FRM-01) or the lead form.
- Record the consent, the date and the channel in the guest profile.
- If a guest says "stop", "unsubscribe" or similar, reply once to confirm, label them **Do not market** and remove them from all lists the same day.
- Never add a guest to a WhatsApp group or broadcast list without consent. Never share one guest's number with another.
- Do not buy or import contact lists.

## 9. Escalation

- Complaints by message: acknowledge within 15 minutes and follow AUR-SAF-05.
- Questions about health conditions: do not advise; send the forms link and offer the Healer consultation; suggest the guest speaks with their doctor.
- Media, influencer or partnership enquiries: pass to the General Manager.
