## 3. Display standard

- The retail display is near the lounge exit, where guests pass after their rest [location to confirm].
- Dust and face up at opening and handover (AUR-SOP-01). Fill gaps from the stockroom straight away.
- Every product has a clear, branded price label showing AED [___] (or a shelf label for each line).
- Group by category. Hero product at eye level. No more than [3] of each item on display.
- Fragrance testers: one per scent, labelled "Tester", with blotter strips. **Low-scent rule:** testers stay capped; no spraying into the air.
- Damaged or dusty items are removed from display.
- Display follows the brand guidelines (AUR-GOV-06): calm, uncluttered, natural materials.

## 4. How we sell: no pressure, ever

Our guests come to calm their nervous system. A sales push undoes that.

- **Wait for the right moment:** only after the post-session rest, never before or during a session.
- **Link it to the guest:** mention a product only if it connects to something the guest said or enjoyed.
- **One mention only.** If the guest says no, or does not respond, let it go.
- No discount talk to close a sale. No "today only". No follow-up messages about retail unless the guest agreed to marketing (AUR-SOP-03).
- Never make health claims about any product.

## 6. Receiving stock

1. Check the delivery against the purchase order (AUR-FIN-10): item, quantity, condition.
1. Check for damage, missing items and correct branding.
1. Note any shortage or damage on the delivery note before signing. Photograph damaged goods.
1. Enter received quantities into [POS / stock sheet] the same day.
1. Store in the stockroom: clean, dry, off the floor, away from cleaning chemicals and laundry. Fragrances away from heat and sunlight.
1. File the delivery note for Finance.
