# Guest Journey & Front-Desk SOP

> Document No. AUR-SOP-02 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

## 1. Purpose

This SOP describes every step of a guest's journey at The Aura Reset Center, from the moment they click an ad or send a WhatsApp message to the moment they leave and receive a follow-up. It gives Hosts timings and short scripts so every guest has the same calm, unhurried experience.

Our promise is **a host, not a receptionist**: someone who knows the modalities walks with the guest from arrival to the last exhale, every visit. Our first value is **the nervous system first**: every visit starts by asking what state the guest arrived in.

Related documents: AUR-SOP-03 (lead response and WhatsApp), AUR-SOP-04 (Healer consultation), AUR-CLI-01 (service menu), AUR-CLI-02 (booking and cancellation), AUR-FRM-01 (intake form), AUR-FRM-02 (health questionnaire), AUR-FRM-03 (screening checklist), AUR-SAF-03 (data protection).

## 3. Stage 1 — Lead or enquiry

Most new guests come from paid ads on Meta, Instagram and Google, and from WhatsApp. Speed matters: a warm, quick reply is the start of the experience.

- Answer every new lead within **5 minutes** during opening hours, using the templates in AUR-SOP-03.
- Record the lead in the lead tracking table (AUR-SOP-03) with its source.
- Ask one gentle question about what they are looking for, using the goals from our "Find your reset" picker: Sleep deeper, Calm the stress, Recover faster, More energy, Skin & glow, Sharper focus, Beat jet lag.

> **Script:** "Hello [Name], thank you for reaching out to The Aura. I'm [Host name]. What would you love to feel more of right now: better sleep, less stress, more energy, or something else?"

## 4. Stage 2 — Booking and confirmation

1. Explain that every first visit begins with a consultation with one of our Healers, who designs a programme around the guest.
1. Offer two time options. Check the unit timeline so no single unit is double-booked (AUR-SOP-10).
1. Book in [Booking system — to be chosen]: name, mobile, email, source, modality or "Not sure yet — build my reset", Healer consultation.
1. Send the confirmation (template below) and the intake and health forms link.
1. Mention cancellation terms briefly and link to AUR-CLI-02. Remind them: no charge until the visit.

**Booking confirmation template**

> Thank you, [Name]. You are booked at The Aura Reset Center on [Day, Date] at [Time]. Your first visit begins with a consultation with one of our Healers, followed by [session / your personalised reset]. Please arrive 10 minutes early, hydrated, and avoid heavy make-up or lotions. No charge until your visit.

**Reminder template (24 hours before)**

> Hello [Name], a gentle reminder of your visit tomorrow, [Date], at [Time]. Arrive 10 minutes early, hydrated. If you need to change anything, just reply here. We look forward to welcoming you. — [Host name], The Aura

## 5. Stage 3 — Before arrival (Host preparation)

- 30 minutes before: check forms are complete. If not, have a tablet ready at the desk.
- Print or open the guest profile. For a first visit, let the Healer know the guest's name and goal.
- Check the unit is ready, clean and at temperature. Lay out robe, branded clothing, towels and slippers in the guest's size if known.

## 6. Stage 4 — Arrival greeting and breathing ritual

Stand up and come out from behind the desk. Greet by name. Keep your voice soft.

> **Script:** "Welcome, [Name]. I'm [Host name], and I'll be looking after you today. Before anything else, let's arrive together. Take four slow breaths with us. In for four, out for six."

Breathe with the guest for four rounds. Then:

> "How are you feeling as you arrive today, on a scale from wired to calm?"

Note the answer in the guest profile. This is the guest's arrival state.

## 7. Stage 5 — Intake and health questionnaire

- Confirm the intake form (AUR-FRM-01) and health questionnaire (AUR-FRM-02) are complete and signed, and the general consent is signed.
- Review answers with the screening checklist (AUR-FRM-03). If anything is flagged, do not book that modality today; explain kindly and offer an alternative. If the answers suggest the guest should speak with their doctor first, say so.
- Never give a medical opinion.

> **Script if something is flagged:** "Thank you for being so open. For this one, we'd like you to check with your doctor first. Our modalities are wellness services and not a substitute for medical care. In the meantime, [alternative] would be a lovely place to start today."

Forms are personal data. Keep tablets locked and screens turned away from other guests (AUR-SAF-03).

## 8. Stage 6 — First-visit Healer consultation

Every guest's **first visit** starts with a consultation with one of our Healers. The full method is in AUR-SOP-04.

**Purpose:** to understand the guest's core concern, how their nervous system is presenting today, and to design a personalised package of sessions.

**Flow:**

1. Host walks the guest to the consultation room and introduces the Healer by name. Offers water or tea.
1. Healer runs the consultation ([30–45 min proposed]) using the intake, health questionnaire and the Host's arrival-state note.
1. Healer recommends a package (modalities, sequence, frequency, breathwork or sound healing) and today's first session.
1. **Handover to Host:** the Healer walks the guest back to the Host and summarises today's plan in front of the guest.

> **Healer handover script:** "[Host name], [Name] would like to start today with [session]. We have talked about a programme to [goal] over the next [__] weeks, and I've added the detail to [Name]'s profile."

**Record the package.** The package recommendation is **recorded in the guest profile** in [Booking system — to be chosen] before the guest leaves: core goal, recommended sessions, sequence, frequency, review date, price quoted as AED [___], and whether the guest accepted.

Returning guests skip the consultation unless they have a scheduled review, a change in health, or ask for one.

## 9. Stage 7 — Amenities and refreshment

Issue:

- a robe in the right size;
- Aura-branded clothing to wear in the centre (top and bottoms, sizes per AUR-SOP-05);
- Aura-branded towels;
- hotel-style Aura-branded slippers;
- a locker key or band.

> **Script:** "Here is everything you need. Your locker is number [__]. Please change into whichever feels comfortable for [session] and I'll meet you here in five minutes. Can I make you a herbal tea or infused water for afterwards?"

Note: some sessions have clothing rules. Follow the modality SOP and Elevé guidance [per Elevé protocol — pending]. The dry float bed is used fully clothed.

## 10. Stage 8 — Session(s)

- Walk the guest to the unit. Explain what will happen, how long it lasts and how to signal you.
- Stay with or close to the guest as the modality SOP requires. The cold plunge is **always Host-guided**.
- Follow the treatment SOP for the unit. Never shorten cleaning or reset time between guests.

### Suggested combinations

Hosts suggest combinations based on the Healer's plan, the guest's state and what is free on the timeline:

| Combination | Why guests enjoy it |
|---|---|
| Infrared Sauna, then Cold Plunge | Classic contrast of heat and cold |
| Infrared Sauna or Cryotherapy, then Compression Recovery | Warm or reset, then deep leg recovery |
| Salt Cabin, then Dry Float Bed or Breathwork | Open the breath, then go into stillness |
| Red-Light Bed, then Sound Healing | Gentle light, then a calm downshift |
| Guided Breathwork before Hyperbaric Oxygen | Settle first, then rest |

> **Script:** "Many guests like to follow the sauna with a short cold plunge. I'd be with you the whole time. Would you like to try it today, or keep it for next time?"

Always check the screening checklist for each added modality. Never press.

## 11. Stage 9 — Post-session rest and refreshments

- Walk the guest to the lounge. Offer their tea or infused water (AUR-SOP-06), served in a copper mug where suitable.
- Allow at least 10 minutes of quiet. Do not start a sales conversation.
- Ask: "How do you feel now, compared to when you arrived?" Note the answer next to the arrival state.

## 12. Stage 10 — Retail moment (soft, never hard selling)

Mention a product only if it connects to something the guest said or enjoyed.

> **Script:** "You mentioned you sleep lightly. Our wellness pillows are on the shelf by the door if you'd like to have a look on your way out. No rush at all."

One mention only. If the guest is not interested, let it go. See AUR-SOP-07.

## 13. Stage 11 — Payment and rebooking

1. Confirm what was used today and take payment by Apple Pay, card or cash (AUR-SOP-08). Members: confirm the session is recorded against their allowance.
1. Offer the next visit based on the Healer's plan, using the script below.

> **Script:** "Your Healer suggested coming back within [__] days for [session]. Shall I hold [Day] at [Time] for you?"

If the guest is interested in the package or a membership, explain it clearly: what is included and the price AED [___]. Founding-member terms only while places remain (first 100).

## 14. Stage 12 — Farewell and follow-up

- Return locker key. Walk the guest to the door. Thank them by name.

> **Script:** "Thank you for coming in today, [Name]. Drink plenty of water this evening. We'll be here when you need us."

- Send the follow-up message the same evening or next morning:

> Hello [Name], thank you for visiting The Aura today. We hope you're feeling the calm. If you have any questions about your programme, just reply here. Your next visit: [Date / "whenever you're ready"]. — [Host name]

- If the guest agreed to marketing messages, add them to the relevant list. Otherwise, send only service messages (AUR-SOP-03).
- After [2–3 days], send the feedback survey link (AUR-FRM-08).

## 15. Standards for every Host

- Use the guest's name. Speak slowly and softly. Short sentences.
- Never make medical claims. Use wellness language only.
- Never leave the desk and floor unattended. One Host stays on the floor at all times (AUR-SOP-10).
- Record everything in the guest profile the same day.
