# Healer Consultation & Package Design SOP

> Document No. AUR-SOP-04 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

> This SOP is a working draft. The Healer assessment method in section 6 is to be described by the Healing team and approved by the General Manager before use.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Every guest's **first visit** to The Aura Reset Center starts with a consultation with one of our **Healers**. Healers are energy-healing and holistic practitioners on our team. They identify the guest's core concern and design a personalised package to support nervous-system regulation and energy healing.

This SOP covers how a Healer prepares for, runs, documents and follows up the consultation. It applies to all Healers (number of Healers: [__]) and to the Hosts who hand guests to and from the Healer.

## 2. Boundaries — read first

- Healers are **not** medical practitioners. They do **not** diagnose, treat or prescribe, and never suggest a guest stops any medication or medical care.
- Our modalities are wellness services designed to support how a guest feels and functions. They are not a substitute for medical care. We always flag anything that warrants a conversation with the guest's doctor.
- **No medical treatments are offered at launch.** IV Nutrient Therapy is not offered. Do not recommend or promise it.
- Hyperbaric Oxygen is offered as a non-medical wellness session [confirm licensing requirements for hyperbaric with DHA / licensing authority].
- Medical questions go to the guest's own doctor. Concerns about our protocols on medical grounds go through the General Manager to the [Medical Director named on the licence]. The Medical Director is not on the floor.
- Physical contact only with the guest's consent and as described in the Healer method. Follow the Anti-Harassment, Respect & Client Boundaries Policy (AUR-POL-04).

### When to refer to a doctor

Pause package design and suggest the guest speaks with their doctor first if:

- the health questionnaire (AUR-FRM-02) or screening checklist (AUR-FRM-03) flags a condition for any modality;
- the guest describes new, unexplained or worsening symptoms;
- the guest is pregnant, recently had surgery, or has a heart, lung, blood-pressure, seizure or circulation condition;
- the guest describes thoughts of self-harm or a mental-health crisis (follow AUR-SAF-04 and give emergency numbers: Ambulance 998, Police 999).

> **Script:** "Thank you for sharing that with me. Before we add [modality], I'd like you to check with your doctor. In the meantime, we can start with something gentle like breathwork."

## 5. Consultation flow

| Stage | Time (guide) | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | 2 min | Healer introduces themself, explains the consultation and that nothing is medical |
| Arrive | 3 min | A short settling breath together: in for four, out for six |
| Listen | 10–15 min | Guest describes what brought them in, what they want to feel, sleep, stress, energy, recovery |
| Assess | 5–10 min | Energy and nervous-system assessment (section 6) |
| Identify the core issue | 3 min | Healer reflects back the core concern in the guest's own words |
| Design | 5–10 min | Healer proposes the package (section 7) |
| Agree and hand over | 2–5 min | Guest chooses; Healer walks the guest to the Host |

**Opening script**

> "Welcome, [Name]. This time is about you. I'll ask a few questions about how you've been feeling, and then we'll design a plan to help your nervous system find its way back to calm. Nothing here is medical. If anything comes up that's for your doctor, I'll tell you."

**Core-issue questions** (choose, do not interrogate)

- "What made you reach out to us now?"
- "If our sessions worked perfectly for you, what would be different in a month?"
- "How are you sleeping? How do you feel when you wake?"
- "Where do you notice stress in your body?"
- "What usually helps you feel calm?"

## 7. Designing the package

Build from the sessions available at launch, checking each against the screening checklist.

| Modality | Pillar | Duration | Often chosen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whole-Body Cryotherapy | Reset | 3 min | Focus, mood, sleep |
| Cold Plunge (always Host-guided) | Reset | 2–5 min | Calm under stress, alertness |
| Dry Float Bed | Reset | 30 min | Zero-gravity rest |
| Salt Cabin | Reset | 30 min | Breathing, skin, relaxation |
| Guided Breathwork | Reset | 45 min | Downshift from stress, sleep, focus |
| Sound Healing | [Pillar to confirm] | [__ min] | Deep relaxation [Healing team to describe] |
| Red-Light Bed | Heal | 20 min | Skin, recovery, mood |
| Infrared Sauna | Heal | 40 min | Deep sweat, muscle tension |
| Hyperbaric Oxygen | Heal | 60 min | Clarity after travel or burnout |
| Compression Recovery | Heal | 30 min | Soreness, circulation |

**Design principles**

1. **Start gently.** Most first-timers start with the infrared sauna or the red-light bed.
1. **Nervous system first.** A guest who arrives wired is offered calming sessions (breathwork, sound healing, dry float, salt cabin) before intense ones (cryotherapy, cold plunge).
1. **Use known pairings:** cold plunge with infrared sauna; compression with sauna or cryotherapy; salt cabin before dry float or breathwork.
1. **Keep it simple.** A first package usually has 2–3 modalities, a sequence and a frequency, for example "twice a week for four weeks".
1. **Check the timeline.** We have one unit of each machine. Design around real availability.
1. **Fit a product.** Map the package to a Single Reset, a package, or a Restore or Elevate membership, whichever is fairest for the guest.

- State prices as confirmed on the current price list. Never invent or round a price. Until prices are final: AED [___].
- Discounts only within the Roles, Responsibilities & Delegation of Authority document (AUR-GOV-02).
- If the guest declines, record "not now" and book today's session only.
