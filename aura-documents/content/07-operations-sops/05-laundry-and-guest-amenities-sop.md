# Laundry & Guest Amenities SOP

> Document No. AUR-SOP-05 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

## 1. Purpose and scope

Every guest at The Aura Reset Center is given a robe, Aura-branded towels, Aura-branded clothing to wear in the centre and hotel-style Aura-branded slippers. Everything is laundered **in-house** on our own laundry machine(s). This SOP sets par levels, hygiene standards, the presentation standard and how damaged items and branded stock are handled.

It applies to all Hosts and to [in-house attendant / outsourced cleaner — to be decided]. It sits alongside the Cleaning & Sanitation Procedures (AUR-SAF-02) and the Health, Safety & Hygiene Policy (AUR-SAF-01).

## 5. Laundry process

### 5.1 Collection

- Collect used items from rooms, changing rooms and the lounge after every guest and at least every [hour].
- Used items go straight into a lined laundry bag or trolley at the point of use, never on the floor and never carried against the body.
- Items soiled with blood or body fluid go into a separate [water-soluble red] bag and are handled with gloves (AUR-SAF-02 section on body fluid spills).
- Keep used and clean laundry apart at all times. Clean items are never carried in a bag or trolley used for dirty items.

### 5.2 Washing

| Item | Wash temperature | Programme / chemical | Dry |
|---|---|---|---|
| Robes | [60 °C proposed] | [per supplier care label / detergent supplier] | Tumble, medium |
| Towels (all) | [60 °C proposed] | [detergent; no fabric softener on sauna towels] | Tumble, medium-high |
| Guest clothing (Aura-branded) | [40–60 °C per care label] | [detergent; inside out to protect the logo] | Tumble low, or line dry |
| Blankets and covers | [60 °C proposed] | [detergent] | Tumble, medium |
| Body fluid soiled items | [Hot wash, 71 °C or higher, or thermal disinfection programme — confirm] | Separate load | Tumble, high |

- Do not overload the machine. Follow the machine manufacturer's load guide [load capacity __ kg].
- Items must be **fully dry** before folding. Damp linen grows bacteria and smells.
- Low-scent rule: use [unscented or lightly scented] detergent. No strong fragrance on items that go into the salt cabin, hyperbaric chamber or near guests with sensitivities.
- Clean the lint filter after every dryer load. Clean the washer door seal and detergent drawer [weekly].
- Never leave a wet load in the machine overnight unless the closing team uses the delayed-start programme set to finish by opening.

### 5.3 Daily laundry rhythm

| Time | Task | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | Start first load (from previous evening) | Opening Host |
| Through the day | Keep one load running; fold dried loads within 30 minutes | Host not on the floor |
| Handover | Report stock below par (AUR-SOP-01) | Outgoing senior Host |
| Closing | Last load dried, folded and stored; used bags emptied | Closing Hosts |

## 6. Folding and presentation standard

Our standard is a calm, hotel-quality presentation that reflects the brand.

- **Robes:** fold in thirds lengthways, then in half, logo facing up, belt tucked inside. Stack by size, largest at the bottom.
- **Bath towels:** fold in thirds lengthways, then in thirds again, folded edge facing out, logo visible on the front edge.
- **Hand towels:** in thirds, rolled for treatment rooms or flat for changing rooms.
- **Guest clothing:** top folded with logo centred on the front; bottoms folded in thirds; set placed together with size tag visible.
- **Slippers:** placed in pairs, toes facing the guest, on a clean towel or tray.
- Shelves are clean, dry and dust-free. No more than [__] items per stack. Clean stock is stored covered or in closed cabinets, at least [15 cm] off the floor.
- In the guest's room or changing area, lay out: robe on hook or folded on the bench, towels on the shelf, clothing set beside the robe, slippers on the floor in front.

## 7. Inspection, damage and stains

Inspect every item as you fold.

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Light stain | Re-wash once with stain treatment [product] |
| Stain remains after re-wash | Remove from guest use; move to "rag" stock (cleaning use) |
| Make-up, oil or lotion marks | Pre-treat, then wash; check before re-issue |
| Tears, pulled threads, holes | Remove from guest use; log in the damage log |
| Faded or cracked logo | Remove from guest use; log |
| Item lost or taken by a guest | Log; inform the General Manager if a pattern |
