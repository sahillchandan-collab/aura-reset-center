# Conflict of Interest, Gifts & Anti-Bribery

> Document No. AUR-POL-05 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: CEO · Effective: [Date]

> Draft for internal use. To be reviewed by a UAE-qualified legal adviser once the trade license is issued.

## 1. Purpose

Guests trust that a Host recommends a protocol because it is right for their nervous system that day, not because someone benefits. This policy keeps decisions at The Aura Reset Center honest and clear. It applies to all employees, managers, contractors and the leadership team, and supports UAE law on bribery and corruption, including the Federal Penal Code.

## 2. What is a conflict of interest

A conflict of interest arises when your personal interests, or those of family or close friends, could influence, or appear to influence, your work decisions. Having a conflict is not wrongdoing. Hiding one is.

Examples at The Aura:

- Recommending a guest to a private practitioner you work with.
- Influencing a supplier choice (linen, salt, Epsom salt for the float tank, IV consumables, cleaning products) where a relative owns the supplier.
- Approving a discount, guest pass or founding-member slot for a friend.
- Managing a rota or appraisal for someone you are in a relationship with.

## 5. The Elevé Health relationship

Elevé Health is our sole technology partner. Every machine at The Aura is Elevé, and The Aura is Elevé Health's first partner clinic in the UAE. Elevé installs, calibrates and services every unit, trains every Host and supports us for life.

Because this is a close and exclusive relationship:

- Commercial terms with Elevé are handled only by the Founder & CEO and CEO.
- Training, travel or hospitality provided by Elevé (for example, visits to the Longevity Lounge in Newport Beach) is accepted only when approved in writing by the CEO and recorded in the register.
- Staff must not accept personal payments, commissions or referral fees from Elevé or its distributors, including for guests who buy equipment for home use.
- Staff must not represent Elevé, sell Elevé products privately, or work for Elevé while employed by The Aura without written approval.
- Service and calibration records are reported accurately. Never sign off an Elevé service visit you did not witness.

## 6. Anti-bribery

The company prohibits bribery in any form.

- You must not offer, promise, give, request or accept any payment, gift or advantage to improperly influence a decision.
- This includes officials at licensing authorities, the Dubai Health Authority, Dubai Municipality, Dubai Civil Defence, MOHRE, immigration and free-zone or mainland departments. Never offer anything to speed up a licence, inspection, visa or approval.
- Facilitation payments ("speed money") are not permitted, even if small.
- Only official government fees with an official receipt are paid.
- Charitable donations or sponsorships in the company's name need Founder & CEO approval.
- Any request for a bribe must be refused and reported to the CEO immediately.

Bribery is gross misconduct and may also be a criminal offence. The company will cooperate with authorities.

## 7. Personal relationships

- Declare any family or romantic relationship with another team member, a supplier, or Elevé staff working with The Aura.
- You must not manage, approve pay or leave for, or investigate, someone with whom you have a close personal relationship. The General Manager will adjust reporting lines.
- Relationships with guests you serve are not permitted (AUR-POL-04). If you have a pre-existing relationship with a guest, declare it and another Host will be assigned.
- Family and friends visiting as guests pay the standard rate of AED [___] or use the staff benefit policy: [Staff and family benefit]. You may not process their booking or apply discounts yourself.

## 8. Outside work

- Outside paid work requires written approval from the General Manager before you start. Approval will not be unreasonably withheld for work that does not compete or affect your rota.
- **Offering private sessions to guests is prohibited.** This includes breathwork, massage, coaching, energy work, home visits, or any wellness service offered to a person you met through The Aura, during or within [12] months after your employment, paid or unpaid.
- Do not use The Aura's name, client list, Elevé protocols, training materials or premises for outside work.
- Do not recruit guests or members to another studio or business.
- Teaching, writing or speaking about wellness in a personal capacity needs approval if it refers to The Aura (see AUR-POL-06).
