# Dress Code & Grooming Standards

> Document No. AUR-POL-03 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

> Draft for internal use. To be reviewed by a UAE-qualified legal adviser once the trade license is issued.

## 1. Purpose

How we look is part of how a guest's nervous system reads the room. A calm, clean, consistent team signals safety before a word is spoken. This standard applies to everyone working at the Jumeirah 1 studio, including contractors on the floor. It is designed for a warm-toned, low-scent, quiet space and for modesty appropriate to Dubai.

## 2. General principles

- Clean, pressed, complete uniform on every shift. No personal clothing visible on the floor.
- Modest: shoulders, upper arms and knees covered for all staff. No sheer fabrics, low necklines or tight fits.
- Low scent: nothing that competes with the salt cabin aromatherapy or affects guests during breathwork.
- Quiet: no clicking heels, jangling jewellery or noisy fabrics.
- Practical: clothing and footwear safe around water, cold surfaces, heat and pressurised chambers.

## 6. Personal hygiene

- Shower before each shift. Use unscented or very lightly scented deodorant.
- Clean, fresh breath. No eating strong-smelling food in uniform before guest contact.
- Wash hands before and after every guest contact, after cleaning and after breaks, in line with Dubai Municipality hygiene rules. Use the sanitiser provided between rooms.
- Do not work with guests if you have a contagious illness, cough, fever, open wound or skin infection. Tell the duty lead (see AUR-POL-02).

## 7. Grooming

### 7.1 Hair

- Clean, neat and tidy. Hair longer than shoulder length is tied back on the floor.
- Natural or subtle colours.
- Facial hair is neatly trimmed or clean shaven. Clinical staff follow any DHA infection-control requirements.

### 7.2 Nails

- Short, clean and filed. Nails must not extend beyond the fingertip.
- Hosts, housekeeping and clinical staff: no acrylics, gels or extensions; these trap bacteria and can catch on equipment, compression boots and guest skin. Clear or nude polish only, without chips.
- Front-of-house: natural or neutral polish, short length.

### 7.3 Make-up

Natural and minimal. Avoid heavy foundation, glitter or strong lipstick, which can transfer onto robes and towels and does not suit our calm look.

## 8. Fragrance

Fragrance is a safety and comfort matter at The Aura. The salt cabin combines halotherapy with aromatherapy, breathwork guests are breathing deeply, and float guests are in sensory reduction.

- No perfume, cologne, oud, bakhoor or strongly scented body products on shift.
- Unscented or very lightly scented hair and skin products only.
- Smoking or vaping odour on clothing or hands is not acceptable. Smoking is not permitted in uniform anywhere visible to guests.
- If a guest or colleague comments on a scent, the duty lead may ask you to change or wash.

## 9. Jewellery and accessories

- Hosts, housekeeping and clinical staff: a plain wedding band, small stud earrings and a simple religious item worn under the uniform. Nothing that dangles, jangles or can catch.
- Remove all metal jewellery before assisting with cryotherapy, cold plunge or hyperbaric oxygen, following the Elevé safety protocol for each unit.
- Front-of-house: up to one small necklace, one ring per hand and small earrings.
- Watches: plain, quiet, no smart notifications on the floor (see AUR-POL-07). Remove for wet-area tasks.
- No visible facial piercings other than a small nose stud.
- Visible tattoos should be modest and not offensive. The company may ask for covering where appropriate.

## 10. Modesty and cultural respect

- Uniform covers shoulders, upper arms and knees, with no visible underwear or midriff.
- Staff must never change in guest areas. Use the staff room.
- Hosts do not enter the cold plunge, sauna or float with a guest. Where a demonstration is needed, it is given fully clothed.
- During Ramadan, take extra care with modesty and discretion.

## 11. Compliance

Duty leads check uniform and grooming at the start of each shift. A team member who does not meet the standard may be asked to correct it before going on the floor. Repeated breaches are handled under the disciplinary procedure in AUR-POL-02. Reasonable adjustments for religious, medical or disability reasons are agreed with the General Manager.
