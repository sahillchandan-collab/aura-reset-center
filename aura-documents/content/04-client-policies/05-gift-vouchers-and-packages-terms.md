## 3. Gift voucher terms

1. **Validity:** **12 months** from the date of issue [proposed]. The expiry date is printed on the voucher.
1. **Not cash:** vouchers cannot be exchanged for cash, in whole or part, and no change is given. Any unused balance on a value voucher stays on the voucher until expiry.
1. **Redemption:** quote the voucher code when booking. Bookings made with a voucher follow our normal booking and cancellation policy. A late cancellation or no-show uses the voucher session.
1. **Price changes:** a modality voucher is honoured for the named modality even if the price changes during validity.
1. **Transfer:** a gift voucher may be passed to another person before it is first redeemed. Once redeemed in part, the balance stays with the person who redeemed it.
1. **Lost or stolen vouchers:** we can reissue a voucher if the purchaser provides proof of purchase or the voucher code, provided it has not been redeemed. We cannot reissue a voucher that has already been used.
1. **Extensions:** one extension of up to [3 months] may be granted before expiry for travel or health reasons, at the General Manager's discretion.
1. **Refunds:** a purchaser may cancel an unredeemed voucher within [14 days] of purchase for a full refund [proposed]. Otherwise vouchers are not refundable, except as required by law.
1. **Combining:** up to [2] vouchers may be used for one booking. Vouchers cannot be used with other promotions unless stated.
1. **Minimum age:** recipients must be 18 or over [proposed]. Under-18s may redeem a voucher for limited modalities with guardian consent (see AUR-CLI-06).
1. **Fraud:** vouchers that are copied, altered or obtained unlawfully are void.
