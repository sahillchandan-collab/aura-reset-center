## Welcome to The Aura

The Aura Reset Center is a recovery studio for the modern nervous system. We bring advanced wellness technology and holistic practice together in one beautiful space, where every experience is created with intention.

Every machine in our Jumeirah 1 studio is from Elevé Health. We are Elevé Health's first partner clinic in the UAE. Each unit is installed by Elevé, calibrated to clinical specification and supported for life. Every host is trained by Elevé. We have one of each machine, so booking ahead is the best way to secure your preferred time.

You will not meet a receptionist here. You will meet a host: someone who knows the modalities and walks with you from arrival to the last exhale.

We are open 7 days a week, 9:00am to 9:00pm.

## How a visit works

1. **Request a time.** Book online at [Website] or on WhatsApp at [WhatsApp]. Choose a modality, or a goal, and a preferred time band: Morning 9am–12pm, Midday 12–3pm, Afternoon 3–6pm or Evening 6–9pm.
1. **Receive your confirmation.** We confirm by email with a calendar invite. No charge until your visit.
1. **Arrive.** Come 10 minutes early, hydrated. Your host welcomes you with a refreshment and asks what state you arrived in.
1. **Meet your Healer (first visit).** Your first visit begins with a consultation with one of our Healers, who identifies the core issue and designs a personalised package to regulate your nervous system and support energy healing.
1. **Breathe.** Every visit begins with our breathing ritual: in for four, out for six.
1. **Reset.** Your session runs with your host close by. Your host suggests the combination of modalities that suits you on the day.
1. **Integrate.** A few quiet minutes, a refreshment, and a short conversation about what comes next.

Not sure where to begin? Book **"Not sure yet — build my reset"** and your host will build a 3-step sequence for you, guided by your Healer's recommendations. Most first-timers start with the infrared sauna or the red-light bed.

## The three pillars

- **Reset** — shift state. Cold, stillness, salt and breath to interrupt stress and return you to calm.
- **Heal** — support recovery. Light, heat, pressure and oxygen to help the body do its quiet repair work.
- **Elevate** — whole person. Healer guidance and sound to support energy, spirit and the nervous system.

## Good to know

- **Arrive 10 minutes early, hydrated.** A late start shortens your session; it cannot be extended.
- **Bring nothing.** We provide robes, Aura-branded towels, Aura-branded clothing to wear in the centre, hotel-style Aura-branded slippers and everything each modality needs.
- **Refreshments.** Herbal teas, infused water and other refreshments are served during your visit [menu to confirm].
- **Skin.** Please avoid heavy make-up and lotions before light and cold sessions.
- **Shower first.** A shower is required before the cold plunge.
- **Health screen.** Some modalities need a short health questionnaire before your first session. Please tell your host about pregnancy, heart conditions, recent surgery or anything you are unsure of.
- **Wellness, not medicine.** Our modalities are wellness services designed to support how you feel and function. They are not a substitute for medical care, and no medical treatments are offered at launch. We will always flag anything that warrants a conversation with your doctor.
