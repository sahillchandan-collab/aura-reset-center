## Welcome to The Aura

The Aura Reset Center is a recovery studio for the modern nervous system. We bring advanced wellness technology and holistic practice together in one beautiful space, where every experience is created with intention.

Every machine in our Jumeirah 1 studio is from Elevé Health. We are Elevé Health's first partner clinic in the UAE. Each unit is installed by Elevé, calibrated to clinical specification and supported for life. Every host is trained by Elevé.

You will not meet a receptionist here. You will meet a host: someone who knows the modalities and walks with you from arrival to the last exhale.

## How a visit works

1. **Request a time.** Choose a modality, or a goal, and a preferred time band: Morning 8–11am, Midday 11am–2pm, Afternoon 2–5pm or Evening 5–8pm.
1. **Receive your confirmation.** We confirm by email with a calendar invite. No charge until your visit.
1. **Arrive.** Come 10 minutes early, hydrated. Your host asks what state you arrived in and tailors your protocol.
1. **Breathe.** Every visit begins with our breathing ritual: in for four, out for six.
1. **Reset.** Your session runs with your host close by.
1. **Integrate.** A few quiet minutes, water, and a short conversation about what comes next.

Not sure where to begin? Book **"Not sure yet — build my reset"** and your host will build a 3-step sequence for you. Most first-timers start with the infrared sauna or the red-light bed.

## The three pillars

- **Reset** — shift state. Cold, stillness, salt and breath to interrupt stress and return you to calm.
- **Heal** — support recovery. Light, heat, pressure and oxygen to help the body do its quiet repair work.
- **Elevate** — replenish. Clinician-administered nutrient therapy to support hydration and how you function.

## Good to know

- **Arrive 10 minutes early, hydrated.** A late start shortens your session; it cannot be extended.
- **Bring nothing.** Robes, towels, slippers and everything each modality needs are provided.
- **Skin.** Please avoid heavy make-up and lotions before light and cold sessions.
- **Shower first.** A shower is required before the float tank and the cold plunge.
- **Health screen.** Some modalities need a short health questionnaire before your first session. Please tell your host about pregnancy, heart conditions, recent surgery or anything you are unsure of.
- **Wellness, not medicine.** Our modalities are wellness services designed to support how you feel and function. They are not a substitute for medical care. IV therapy is administered by licensed clinicians, and we will always flag anything that warrants a conversation with your doctor.
- **Minimum age.** 18 [proposed]. Under-18s may use a limited set of modalities with guardian consent.
