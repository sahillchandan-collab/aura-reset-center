# Refund, Rescheduling & Service Recovery Policy

> Document No. AUR-CLI-03 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

> Draft for internal use. To be reviewed by a UAE-qualified legal adviser once the trade license is issued.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out when The Aura Reset Center gives refunds, credits or replacement sessions, and how hosts put things right when an experience falls short. It sits alongside the Booking, Arrival, Cancellation & No-Show Policy (AUR-CLI-02), the Membership Terms & Conditions (AUR-CLI-04) and the Gift Vouchers & Packages Terms (AUR-CLI-05). Nothing in this policy limits a guest's rights under UAE consumer protection law.

## 2. Principles

- **Make it right first.** Our first response to a problem is a calm conversation and a practical fix, not a form.
- **Credit before refund, unless a refund is fairer.** A replacement session or account credit is usually the quickest remedy. Where a guest prefers a refund and is entitled to one, we refund.
- **Safety never costs the guest.** A session stopped for safety is never charged.
- **Consistency.** Hosts follow the matrix in section 4. The General Manager approves anything outside it.

## 3. Definitions

- **Credit:** a value or a session added to the guest's account, valid for [12 months] [proposed], usable for any modality of equal value.
- **Replacement session:** the same modality rebooked at no charge.
- **Refund:** money returned to the original payment method.

## 4. When refunds, credits and replacements apply

| Situation | Remedy | Who approves |

| Cancelled by the guest outside the cancellation window | Full refund of any deposit, or no charge | Host |

## 5. Equipment failure

Every machine is Elevé Health equipment, installed by Elevé, calibrated to clinical specification and serviced on Elevé's clinical service schedule. Even so, faults can occur.

- If a unit faults, the host stops the session, makes sure the guest is safe and comfortable, and records the fault in the Equipment Fault Log [reference].
- The host offers the guest, in this order: an alternative modality that day (if suitable), a replacement session, or a full refund.
- For a member, a session lost to equipment failure is never counted against their allowance and is reinstated.
- The General Manager contacts Elevé Health technical support and closes the unit until it is cleared.
- Where several guests are affected (for example, a unit out of service for a day), we contact all booked guests in advance and offer rebooking or a refund.

## 6. Sessions stopped for safety

Hosts, Healers and facilitators may stop any session at any time for safety, for example a reading on the cryotherapy thermal-vision monitor, discomfort during hyperbaric pressurisation, dizziness in the sauna, or breathlessness in the cold plunge.

- The guest is cared for first. Incidents are recorded under our incident reporting procedure [reference].
- The session is not charged. Members have the session reinstated.
- If the stop arose because the guest did not disclose relevant health information, the session is still not charged, but we may require a doctor's note before that modality is booked again.
- If the stop arose because the guest did not follow host instructions or the House Rules (AUR-CLI-06), the General Manager decides whether a charge applies.

## 7. Rescheduling initiated by The Aura

If we need to move a booking (equipment service, Healer or facilitator availability, power or water interruption, building works or events outside our control), we will:

1. contact the guest as early as possible by [Phone / WhatsApp / Email];
1. offer the nearest suitable slot, an alternative modality or a credit;
1. refund any deposit or prepaid amount in full if the guest prefers.

## 12. Service recovery: how hosts put things right

When a guest is unhappy, the host:

1. **Listens.** Finds a quiet place, gives the guest full attention, does not argue or explain away.
1. **Acknowledges.** Thanks the guest and names what went wrong.
1. **Acts.** Offers the remedy in section 4 that fits, within the host's authority, on the spot where possible.
1. **Records.** Logs the matter in the Guest Feedback Log [reference] the same day.
1. **Follows up.** The host or General Manager contacts the guest within [48 hours].

Hosts may offer goodwill gestures up to [a replacement session / AED [___] credit] without approval. Anything above this goes to the General Manager.

Guests who are not satisfied may write to the General Manager at [Email]. We acknowledge within [2] working days and aim to resolve within [10] working days. ## 14. UAE consumer protection

This policy is intended to be consistent with UAE Federal consumer protection law and Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism consumer rights guidance. Guests keep any statutory rights they have, including the right to receive a service as described and to a remedy where it is not. If a guest is not satisfied with our response, they may contact the Dubai consumer protection authority [contact reference]. Refund and cancellation terms are shown to guests at booking, on the confirmation email and at the studio.
