# Guest Complaints & Feedback Handling Procedure

> Document No. AUR-SAF-05 · Version 0.1 (Draft) · Owner: General Manager · Effective: [Date]

## 1. Purpose

A host walks with the guest from arrival to the last exhale. That includes the moments when something has not felt right. This procedure explains how The Aura Reset Center listens to feedback, resolves complaints quickly and fairly, and learns from them.

It applies to every Host, clinician, Duty Manager and the General Manager at the Jumeirah 1 studio, from the founding member launch in November 2026 onward.

## 2. Principles

- **Listen first.** A complaint is a guest telling us what they need. Thank them for it.
- **Calm and warm.** Lower your voice, slow your breath, and stay present. Never argue or defend.
- **Own it.** The person who hears the complaint owns it until it is resolved or handed over by name.
- **Fair and consistent.** Similar issues get similar outcomes, within the authority levels below.
- **Confidential.** Complaint details are shared only with those who need them (see AUR-SAF-03).
- **Safety first.** Any complaint about injury, an adverse reaction or a device fault is also an incident and follows AUR-SAF-04.
- **Learn.** Every complaint is logged and reviewed for its root cause.

## 5. Service recovery: the LEARN steps

| Step | What the Host does | Example words |
|---|---|---|
| **L — Listen** | Give full attention. Let the guest finish. Take notes if needed. Move somewhere private and calm. | "Please, tell me what happened." |
| **E — Empathise** | Acknowledge how they feel, without blame or excuses. | "I can see that wasn't the experience you came for. I'm sorry." |
| **A — Apologise and Act** | Apologise sincerely. Agree what you will do, within your authority. | "Let me sort this for you now." |
| **R — Resolve** | Fix it now if you can, or give a clear time and name for the next step. Check the guest is happy with the outcome. | "I've rebooked you for Thursday at 5pm. Does that work for you?" |
| **N — Notify** | Log the complaint and tell the Duty Manager. Follow up with the guest if promised. | |

Never promise what you cannot deliver. Never discuss another guest. If the guest is upset or unwell, care for them first; the complaint can wait.

## 6. Authority levels for goodwill gestures

| Level | Who | May offer (examples) | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Host | Sincere apology; complimentary water or tea; extend or repeat part of a session if schedule allows; rebook at no penalty | Up to AED [___] value |
| 2 | Duty Manager / Senior Host | Complimentary single session (Single Reset); a guest pass; waive a late-cancellation fee | Up to AED [___] value |
| 3 | General Manager | Credit to account; partial or full refund of a visit; membership extension or pause; IV therapy credit | Up to AED [___] value |
| 4 | Founder & CEO or CEO | Anything above Level 3; any matter with legal, clinical or media risk | No set limit |

All gestures are recorded in the complaint log. A goodwill gesture is not an admission of liability. Refunds always follow the Refund, Rescheduling & Service Recovery Policy (AUR-CLI-03).

## 7. Escalation

1. **Host** resolves within Level 1.
2. If the guest is not satisfied, or it is beyond Level 1, the **Duty Manager** takes over, ideally on the same visit.
3. If unresolved, or for any complaint about billing disputes, staff conduct, discrimination or a repeat issue, the **General Manager** handles it personally.
4. **Clinical complaints** (IV Nutrient Therapy, or medical concerns from any modality) go to the **Clinical Lead** and General Manager. Where DHA rules require, the guest is informed of their right to complain to DHA, subject to DHA requirements.
5. Complaints with legal, safety, media or reputational risk go to the **Founder & CEO and CEO** within [24 hours].
6. Device-related complaints are shared with **Elevé Health** for technical review.

If a guest is still unhappy after the General Manager's final response, they may contact [Consumer protection authority, e.g. Dubai Economy and Tourism consumer protection] or DHA for clinical matters.

## 8. Root-cause review

- The General Manager reviews the complaint log every [month] and shares themes at the team meeting.
- For each serious or repeated complaint, ask "why" until the real cause is found: booking system, training, device, cleaning, communication, or scheduling.
- Actions are assigned with an owner and date, and checked at the next review.
- A quarterly summary goes to the Founder & CEO and CEO: number of complaints, categories, response times, gestures given, and improvements made.
- Compliments are logged too, and shared with the Host named.

## 9. Common complaint categories

Use these in the log so trends are easy to see:

- Booking and scheduling (time bands, confirmation, calendar invite)
- Waiting time or rushed session
- Host experience
- Modality or device (comfort, temperature, fault)
- Cleanliness and amenities
- IV Nutrient Therapy (clinical)
- Membership and billing (Restore, Elevate, founding member, pause or cancel)
- Facilities (changing rooms, showers, noise, parking)
- Privacy or data
- Other
