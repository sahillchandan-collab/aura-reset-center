## 1. Purpose and scope

Guests trust us with how they feel, what they are recovering from and, sometimes, their medical history. This policy explains how The Aura Reset Center collects, uses, protects and shares personal data, and how every team member keeps guest information confidential.

It applies to all personal data processed by [Registered Company Name] ([Trade License No.]) for the Dubai studio in Jumeirah 1, in any format: paper, digital, audio and video. It applies to employees, Hosts, Healers, contractors and anyone with access to our systems.

## 2. Legal framework

- **UAE:** Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL) and its executive regulations as issued. No medical treatments are offered at launch, so no clinical records are created. Wellness intake data, and any information shared with the [Medical Director named on the licence] for escalation, is handled in line with UAE health data laws and DHA requirements where applicable. If clinical services (for example IV Nutrient Therapy) are added in future, subject to DHA licensing, this policy would be updated for clinical record keeping.
- **United States:** our location at 1936 East Deere Avenue, Suite 120, Santa Ana, California handles its own guest data under applicable US federal and California law. Where a member uses both locations ("your membership travels with you"), data shared between them is treated as an international transfer (Section 11).

[Registered Company Name] is the controller for Dubai guest data. Data Protection Lead: [Name / role, e.g. General Manager], [Email].

## 3. Principles

We process personal data:

1. Lawfully, fairly and transparently.
2. Only for clear, stated purposes.
3. Limited to what we actually need.
4. Accurately, and corrected when wrong.
5. Kept no longer than needed.
6. Securely, protected against loss, misuse and unauthorised access.
7. With accountability: we can show how we comply.

## 8. Staff confidentiality

Every team member signs a confidentiality agreement before starting. In practice:

- Never discuss a guest's health, visit or conversation outside the studio, on social media, or with other guests.
- Never confirm whether someone is a guest. Many guests are well known; discretion is part of our service.
- Do not photograph or film guests. Social content with a guest requires their written consent.
- Speak quietly about guest details in shared areas. Use names only, not conditions, at reception.
- Share with colleagues only what they need to serve the guest safely.
- Breach of confidentiality is a disciplinary matter under our HR policies and UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021.

## 9. Data subject rights

Under the PDPL, guests may ask to:

- be informed how their data is used
- access a copy of their data
- correct inaccurate data
- delete their data (subject to legal retention, e.g. tax records)
- restrict or object to processing
- move their data to another provider (portability)
- withdraw consent at any time
- object to direct marketing

How we respond:

1. Requests come by [Email], in person or by [WhatsApp]. Any team member who receives one passes it to the Data Protection Lead the same day.
2. We verify the person's identity.
3. We respond within [30] days or the timeframe set by the PDPL regulations.
4. We log each request: date, type, action taken, response date.
5. No fee, unless the request is manifestly excessive.

## 10. Data breach response

A breach is any loss, theft, unauthorised access or disclosure of personal data, for example a misdirected email, a lost tablet, or intake forms left at reception.

1. **Contain:** the person who finds it reports it at once to the Duty Manager and Data Protection Lead. Stop the cause (recall the email, change passwords, lock the account).
2. **Assess:** within [24 hours], the Data Protection Lead assesses what data, how many guests, and the risk to them. Health data breaches are treated as high risk.
3. **Notify:** where required by the PDPL, notify the UAE Data Office within the required timeframe, and notify affected guests without delay where the breach may harm them. Escalate to the General Manager, Founder & CEO and CEO. Breaches involving health data are also reported to the [Medical Director named on the licence] and, where required, DHA.
4. **Record:** log every breach, even minor ones, with facts, effects and actions.
5. **Learn:** review the cause and update controls and training.
